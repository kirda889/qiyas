شرح التقويم 1: الأنواع والمبادئ (Assessment I: Types & Principles)

أصول تدريس اللغةالمعيار 6.9.3التخصصي English 2 1448

Know test types by purpose, formative vs summative, and Brown's five principles — especially washback.أنواع الاختبارات ومبادئها

Testing, assessment, evaluationالقياس والتقويم والتقييم

  • A test is a formal procedure; assessment is an ongoing process of gathering information.اختبار / تقويم
  • Evaluation determines the merit and worth of a learning process or programme.التقييم حكم على البرنامج
  • Assessment information is used to guide teaching and modify programmes according to students' needs.معلومات التقويم توجّه التدريس
مصطلحات القسم (3)
Test
A formal procedure for measuring ability or knowledge.اختبار
Assessment
The ongoing process of collecting information about learning.تقويم
Evaluation
A systematic judgment of the merit, value and worth of a programme or process.تقييم

Formative vs summativeتكويني وختامي

  • Formative: during learning; ongoing feedback without interrupting instruction (discussions, pop quizzes, exit tickets).تكويني أثناء التعلم
  • Summative: at the end of a unit, term or programme; usually formal tests.ختامي في النهاية
  • Pop quizzes roughly monitor progress and keep students on their toes.اختبارات مفاجئة
  • Diagnostic assessment is needed prior to instruction.التشخيصي قبل التدريس
  • A teacher observing a role-play = informal assessment.ملاحظة = غير رسمي
مصطلحات القسم (5)
Formative assessment
Ongoing assessment during learning to improve it.تكويني
Summative assessment
Assessment at the end of instruction to measure achievement.ختامي
Pop quiz
A short unannounced quiz to check progress.اختبار مفاجئ
Informal assessment
Spontaneous, unplanned assessment such as observation.غير رسمي
Assessment for / of / as learning
Formative / summative / self-assessment & metacognition.من أجل / للتعلم / كتعلم

Test types by purposeأنواع الاختبارات حسب الغرض

  • Proficiency: general ability, not based on a particular course.كفاءة بلا مقرر
  • Placement: assigns suitable levels; creates homogeneous groups.تصنيف: مجموعات متقاربة
  • Diagnostic: identifies strengths and weaknesses.تشخيصي
  • Achievement: learning of a particular course; progress: recently taught material.تحصيلي / مرحلي
  • Aptitude: predicts success in learning a language.استعداد
  • Surveys and questionnaires = needs assessment; course evaluation is an indirect method.احتياجات / غير مباشر
مصطلحات القسم (6)
Proficiency test
Measures general language ability regardless of any course.كفاءة
Placement test
Places learners in the appropriate level or programme.تصنيف
Diagnostic test
Identifies learners' strengths and weaknesses.تشخيصي
Achievement test
Measures how well course objectives were achieved.تحصيلي
Progress test
Measures learning of recently taught material.تقدم
Aptitude test
Predicts a person's success in learning a foreign language.استعداد

Test formatsصيغ الاختبارات

  • Norm-referenced compares test-takers with each other; criterion-referenced compares with set objectives.مرجعي المعيار / المحك
  • Discrete-point tests one element; integrative tests several skills — an essay best evaluates integrated skills.نقطي / تكاملي
  • Objective items have one correct answer and are scored reliably and quickly (e.g. a gap-fill grammar test).موضوعي
  • Deleting words from a paragraph for students to fill in = cloze test.اختبار الإكمال
مصطلحات القسم (7)
Norm-referenced test
Interprets scores relative to other test-takers.مرجعي المعيار
Criterion-referenced test
Interprets scores against fixed criteria or objectives.مرجعي المحك
Discrete-point test
Tests one language element at a time.نقطي
Integrative test
Tests several skills or components together.تكاملي
Objective test
A test with one correct answer per item, scored without judgment.موضوعي
Subjective test
A test scored by rater judgment (essays, interviews).ذاتي
Cloze test
A text with deleted words that learners restore.اختبار الإكمال

Principles of a good testمبادئ الاختبار الجيد

  • Brown's five principles: practicality, reliability, validity, authenticity, washback.المبادئ الخمسة
  • The impact of testing on teaching and learning, negative or positive = washback.الأثر الرجعي
  • A well-prepared exam is valid and reliable; validity is the most important.صادق وثابت، والصدق الأهم
  • Portfolios and projects = alternative / performance-based assessment; portfolios track progress over time.تقويم بديل
مصطلحات القسم (7)
Practicality
Ease of administration in time, cost and scoring.العملية
Reliability
Consistency of results.الثبات
Validity
The degree to which a test measures what it is intended to measure.الصدق
Authenticity
Correspondence of test tasks to real-world language use.الأصالة
Washback (backwash)
The effect of testing on teaching and learning.الأثر الرجعي
Alternative assessment
Portfolios, journals, conferences, self/peer assessment.تقويم بديل
Self-assessment
Learners evaluate their own work.تقويم ذاتي

The CEFR: levels and can-do descriptorsالإطار الأوروبي المرجعي: المستويات وعبارات can-do

  • The CEFR (Council of Europe, 2001; Companion Volume 2020) is a reference framework — not a test; six levels in three bands.إطار مرجعي وليس اختباراً، بستة مستويات
  • Descriptors are positive can-do statements (action-oriented approach); the 2020 update added mediation.عبارات «أستطيع» إيجابية
  • Uses: aligning curricula and tests, writing objectives, and learner self-assessment.مواءمة المناهج والتقييم الذاتي
مصطلحات القسم (6)
CEFR
Common European Framework of Reference for Languages: six proficiency levels from A1 to C2.الإطار الأوروبي المرجعي
Can-do statements
Positive descriptors of what learners can do in a language at a given level.عبارات أستطيع أن
Basic / independent / proficient user
The three CEFR bands: A1–A2, B1–B2, C1–C2.النطاقات الثلاثة
Action-oriented approach
The CEFR view of learners as social agents who use language to complete tasks.منهج موجّه نحو الفعل
Mediation
Helping others understand a text or concept, or bridging between people — a CEFR mode of communication.الوساطة
European Language Portfolio
A document in which learners record language experiences and self-assess against CEFR levels.ملف اللغة الأوروبي

International standardized proficiency testsالاختبارات الدولية المقننة

  • A standardized test uses uniform conditions and scoring, so results compare across test-takers.ظروف وتصحيح موحّد
  • IELTS: Academic and General Training; four skills; band scale of 0–9; speaking is a face-to-face interview.آيلتس: 0–9 ومقابلة
  • TOEFL iBT (ETS): academic English; speaking is recorded, no live examiner; since 21 January 2026 a 1–6 CEFR-aligned scale.توفل: محادثة مسجّلة ومقياس 1–6
  • Cambridge exams target one CEFR level each: A2 Key (KET), B1 Preliminary (PET), B2 First, C1 Advanced, C2 Proficiency.KET = A2 وPET = B1
  • All are high-stakes proficiency tests, so they create strong washback.اختبارات كفاءة عالية التأثير
مصطلحات القسم (6)
Standardized test
A test given and scored in a consistent, predetermined way so scores are comparable.اختبار مقنن
IELTS
International English Language Testing System: Academic and General Training versions, band scores 0–9.آيلتس
TOEFL iBT
ETS's internet-based Test of English as a Foreign Language for academic settings; since January 2026 scored on a 1–6 band scale.توفل
A2 Key (KET)
Cambridge exam at CEFR A2 (Key English Test).اختبار كامبريدج A2
B1 Preliminary (PET)
Cambridge exam at CEFR B1 (Preliminary English Test).اختبار كامبريدج B1
High-stakes test
A test whose results have important consequences (admission, jobs, visas).اختبار عالي المخاطر

Formal and informal formative assessmentالتقويم التكويني الرسمي وغير الرسمي

  • Formal formative: planned, recorded tasks (quizzes, worksheets) marked and returned with feedback.رسمي: مهام مصححة أثناء التعلم
  • Informal formative: on-the-spot checks — observation, questioning, thumbs up/down — usually not graded.غير رسمي: ملاحظة وأسئلة سريعة
مصطلحات القسم (1)
Formal formative assessment
Planned, marked tasks during a unit used to give feedback and guide learning.تقويم تكويني رسمي

Peer assessment and beneficial washbackتقييم الأقران والأثر الرجعي الإيجابي

  • Peer assessment: learners judge a classmate's work against the teacher's criteria; self-assessment: their own work.الأقران / الذات
  • Beneficial (positive) washback: a good test encourages useful learning (a speaking test → more speaking practice).الاختبار يوجه لتعلم مفيد
مصطلحات القسم (2)
Peer assessment
Learners evaluate each other's work using shared criteria.تقييم الأقران
Beneficial washback
A positive effect of a test on classroom teaching and learning.أثر رجعي إيجابي

أشهر الفخاخ في هذا الدرس

  • Testing's positive or negative impact on teaching = washback (very frequent).الأثر الرجعي
  • Proficiency tests are not based on a particular programme.الكفاءة بلا مقرر
  • Placement creates homogeneous (similar-level) groups.متقاربة
