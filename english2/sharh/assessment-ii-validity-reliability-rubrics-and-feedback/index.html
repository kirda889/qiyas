شرح التقويم 2: الصدق والثبات والسلالم والتغذية (Assessment II: Validity, Reliability, Rubrics & Feedback)

أصول تدريس اللغةالمعيار 6.9.3التخصصي English 2 1448

Distinguish types of validity and reliability, analytic vs holistic rubrics, and principles of feedback.أنواع الصدق والثبات والتغذية

Validityالصدق

  • When an assessment fails to measure what it is set for, it lacks validity.يفتقد الصدق
  • Content validity is supported by a table of specifications linking items to objectives and content.جدول المواصفات
  • Construct validity is threatened when scores depend on something else — memory in a long listening test, speed in a reading test.قياس شيء غير المقصود
مصطلحات القسم (6)
Content validity
The test adequately samples course content and objectives.صدق المحتوى
Construct validity
The test measures the theoretical construct it claims to measure.صدق البناء
Face validity
The test appears valid to test-takers.صدق ظاهري
Predictive validity
The test predicts future performance.صدق تنبؤي
Concurrent validity
Results agree with another valid measure given at the same time.صدق تلازمي
Table of specifications
A blueprint linking items to content and objectives.جدول المواصفات

Reliabilityالثبات

  • Reliability: the extent to which a task consistently yields the same results.الثبات
  • Inter-rater reliability is checked by several teachers scoring the same work.أكثر من معلم
  • A test can be reliable without being valid, but not valid without being reliable.الثبات شرط للصدق
مصطلحات القسم (5)
Test–retest reliability
Giving the same test twice to the same learners.إعادة الاختبار
Parallel-forms reliability
Comparing two equivalent versions of a test.صور متكافئة
Internal consistency
Agreement among items (split-half, Cronbach's alpha).اتساق داخلي
Inter-rater reliability
Consistency between different raters.بين المصححين
Intra-rater reliability
Consistency of one rater over time.المصحح نفسه

Rubrics and itemsسلالم التقدير والأسئلة

  • A rubric grades work against criteria; its main benefit is making criteria clear to students.سلّم التقدير يوضح المعايير
  • Analytic: a score per criterion; holistic: one overall score (limitation: no specific feedback).تحليلي / كلي
  • Co-constructing rubrics helps learners view assessment as an effective educational tool.بناؤه مع الطلاب
  • An objective item has a single correct answer; wrong options are distractors.إجابة واحدة ومشتتات
مصطلحات القسم (6)
Rubric
A scoring guide with criteria and performance levels.سلّم تقدير
Analytic rubric
Gives a separate score for each criterion.تحليلي
Holistic rubric
Gives one overall score for the whole performance.كلي
Stem / key / distractors
Question text / correct answer / wrong options.جذع / مفتاح / مشتتات
Item discrimination
An item's ability to distinguish high from low achievers.معامل التمييز
Item facility
The proportion of test-takers answering correctly.معامل السهولة

Feedbackالتغذية الراجعة

  • Feedback informs both students and teachers.للطالب والمعلم
  • Effective feedback is timely, specific, clear, focused, balanced, helpful and impersonal, linked to objectives.مبادئ التغذية
  • Content feedback addresses ideas; form feedback addresses language accuracy.محتوى / شكل
  • Effective teachers encourage long-term achievement through self-evaluation.التقويم الذاتي
مصطلحات القسم (4)
Feedback
Information given to learners about their performance to guide improvement.تغذية راجعة
Content feedback
Comments on ideas and meaning.على المحتوى
Form feedback
Comments on grammar, vocabulary and mechanics.على الشكل
Constructive alignment
Objectives, activities and assessment aligned (Biggs).مواءمة بنائية

How to test each skillكيف نختبر كل مهارة

  • Direct testing = performing the skill itself; indirect = underlying abilities (e.g. spotting errors to estimate writing).المباشر أداء المهارة
  • Direct tests suit productive skills: they reflect the real skill and create positive washback.المباشر أفضل للإنتاجية
  • Listening: gap-fill, multiple choice, information transfer (map, table, form), dictation — not memory or reading.تحويل المعلومات وتعبئة الفراغ
  • Reading: true/false/not given, matching headings, short answers; items need the text (passage dependence).الأسئلة تعتمد على النص
  • Speaking and writing: scored with a rubric, ideally two raters; writing needs a clear prompt (purpose, audience, length).معيار تصحيح ومصححان
  • Grammar & vocabulary: gap-fill, sentence transformation, word formation, C-test.تحويل وتكوين كلمات وC-test
مصطلحات القسم (5)
Direct test
A test in which candidates perform the actual skill being measured.اختبار مباشر
Indirect test
A test that measures the abilities underlying a skill rather than the skill itself.اختبار غير مباشر
Information transfer
A task where learners move information from a text or recording into a chart, map or form.تحويل المعلومات
Passage dependence
The extent to which reading items can only be answered by reading the passage.الاعتماد على النص
C-test
A reduced-redundancy test in which the second half of every second word is deleted.اختبار C

Analysing test results to improve teachingتحليل النتائج لتحسين التدريس

  • Item facility (IF) = correct ÷ total. 15 of 20 → IF = 0.75; keep items between about 0.15 and 0.85.السهولة = الصحيح ÷ العدد
  • Item discrimination (ID) = (high correct − low correct) ÷ (½ × both groups); e.g. (8−2) ÷ 10 = 0.6.التمييز بين العليا والدنيا
  • ID ranges from −1 to +1; a negative ID signals a faulty item — revise or remove it.التمييز السالب = سؤال معيب
  • Distractor analysis: a distractor nobody chooses isn't working; one attracting high scorers may be ambiguous.مشتت لا يختاره أحد = غير فعال
  • Descriptive statistics: mean, median, mode, range and standard deviation (spread).المتوسط والوسيط والمنوال والانحراف
  • If most of the class fails one objective, reteach it; keep records to plan remediation.إعادة تدريس الهدف الضعيف
مصطلحات القسم (6)
Item analysis
Examining each test item's facility, discrimination and distractors after a test.تحليل الفقرات
Distractor analysis
Checking how many test-takers chose each wrong option and who chose it.تحليل المشتتات
Mean
The arithmetic average of a set of scores.المتوسط الحسابي
Median
The middle score when scores are put in order.الوسيط
Mode
The most frequent score.المنوال
Standard deviation
A measure of how far scores spread out from the mean.الانحراف المعياري

More on testing listening and the C-testالمزيد عن اختبار الاستماع واختبار C

  • Good listening evidence: retelling or summarising a talk — not spelling or reading tasks.التلخيص بعد الاستماع
  • A C-test makes learners read the damaged text, so it draws mainly on reading and overall proficiency.اختبار C يعتمد على القراءة

Scoring tools and item formatsأدوات التصحيح وأشكال الأسئلة

  • Checklist: yes/no, present or absent; rating scale: degree (e.g. 1–5); rubric: criteria + level descriptions.قائمة رصد / سلم تقدير / روبرك
  • A classic cloze deletes every nth word (e.g. every 7th) — a fixed-ratio cloze.حذف منتظم لكل كلمة رقم n
  • Multiple-choice items: quick and objective to score, but allow guessing and don't show productive ability directly.سهل التصحيح لكن فيه تخمين
مصطلحات القسم (3)
Checklist
A list of behaviours marked only as present or absent.قائمة رصد نعم/لا
Rating scale
A scale for judging the degree or frequency of a behaviour (e.g., 1–5).سلم تقدير بالدرجات
Fixed-ratio cloze
A cloze test that deletes every nth word of a passage.حذف كل كلمة رقم n

أشهر الفخاخ في هذا الدرس

  • Inter-rater reliability = several teachers, not student and peer.أكثر من معلم
  • Reliability = consistency; validity = measures what it should.ثبات / صدق
  • Co-constructed rubrics ≠ easier standards; they show assessment as a learning tool.ليس تسهيلاً
