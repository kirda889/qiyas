شرح تدريس الكتابة (Teaching Writing)

Distinguish product, process and genre approaches; know the writing stages, essay types and feedback.مداخل الكتابة ومراحلها وأنواع المقال

Approaches to writingمداخل الكتابة

  • Approaches differ in what they focus on: the final text, the writer's stages, or the text type and its purpose.الناتج أم المراحل أم نوع النص
  • Writing is often the hardest skill: graphic form plus organized thought.أصعب مهارة
مصطلحات القسم (4)
Process approach
A writing approach focusing on the recursive stages of producing a text.مدخل العمليات
Product approach
A writing approach based on imitating model texts and accuracy of the final product.مدخل المنتج
Genre approach
Teaching writing through text types and their purposes.مدخل النوع
Recursive
Writers move back and forth between stages.دائري

Stages of the writing processمراحل الكتابة

  • Peers' and teacher's suggestions = revising; grammar and spelling = editing/proofreading.المراجعة / التدقيق
  • Among 'reviewing, editing, drafting, evaluation', the first stage is drafting.Drafting أولاً بين هذه الخيارات
  • Early-stage writers focus on fluency more than accuracy; nonstop writing = freewriting.الطلاقة أولاً
مصطلحات القسم (8)
Pre-writing
Generating and organizing ideas before writing (brainstorming, clustering, outlining).ما قبل الكتابة
Drafting
Writing a first version focusing on ideas rather than accuracy.المسودة
Revising
Improving content and organization, often using peer/teacher feedback.المراجعة
Editing / Proofreading
Correcting grammar, spelling and punctuation.التدقيق
Publishing
Sharing the final version.النشر
Brainstorming
Freely generating as many ideas as possible about a topic.عصف ذهني
Freewriting
Writing non-stop without worrying about grammar or spelling.كتابة حرة
Clustering / Concept mapping
Organizing related ideas visually around a central topic.تجميع / خريطة مفاهيم

Controlled, guided and free writingمضبوطة وموجهة وحرة

  • Controlled writing: copying, dictation, substitution, rewriting in another tense.مضبوطة: نسخ وإملاء
  • Guided writing: an outline, words and grammatical patterns are given.موجّهة: مخطط وكلمات
  • Young learners start with modeled writing; analyzing a model essay with students = modeling.النمذجة أولاً
مصطلحات القسم (4)
Controlled writing
Highly structured writing where content and form are fixed (e.g. dictation).كتابة مضبوطة
Guided writing
Writing supported by an outline, model or prompts.كتابة موجهة
Free writing
Independent writing with minimal guidance.كتابة حرة
Modeled writing
The teacher writes in front of students, thinking aloud.النمذجة

Paragraphs and essaysالفقرة والمقال

  • Paragraph = topic sentence + supporting sentences + concluding/closing sentence.موضوعية + داعمة + ختامية
  • Essay = introduction (engaging), body paragraphs, conclusion; the broader thesis statement comes at the end of the introduction.الأطروحة آخر المقدمة
  • Transitions (however, moreover) improve coherence; a run-on joins two clauses without punctuation.الانتقال والجملة المتداخلة
مصطلحات القسم (8)
Topic sentence
The sentence stating the main idea of a paragraph.الجملة الموضوعية
Supporting sentences
Sentences that explain and develop the topic sentence.الجمل الداعمة
Concluding (closing) sentence
The final sentence that restates the main idea.الجملة الختامية
Thesis statement
The central claim of an essay; broader than a topic sentence.جملة الأطروحة
Coherence
Logical connection and clarity of ideas in a text.ترابط معنوي
Cohesion
Grammatical and lexical links between sentences.ترابط شكلي
Run-on sentence
Two or more independent clauses joined without correct punctuation.جملة متداخلة
Abstract
A brief summary of a research paper's methods and findings.ملخص البحث

Types of writingأنواع الكتابة

  • Narrative: a story in chronological order; descriptive: sensory details, metaphors.سردي / وصفي
  • Expository: explains or informs (how-to, cause–effect); persuasive/argumentative: a position with facts and statistics.تفسيري / إقناعي
  • Reflective: journal responses; informative: summaries, letters, reports.تأملي / إخباري
  • 'Hi Anas' in a formal letter = wrong register (use 'Dear Sir or Madam').مستوى الخطاب
مصطلحات القسم (6)
Narrative writing
Writing that tells a story, often for pleasure.سردي
Descriptive writing
Writing that creates a picture through sensory details.وصفي
Expository writing
Writing that explains or informs.تفسيري
Persuasive / argumentative writing
Writing that takes a position and supports it with evidence.إقناعي
Reflective writing
Writing that responds to personal experiences, feelings or ideas.تأملي
Voice
The writer's personal expression of opinions in writing.صوت الكاتب

Feedback on writingتصحيح الكتابة

  • Many error areas → mark one grammatical area; a common-errors list helps students revise and rewrite.صحّح مجالاً واحداً
  • Semester drafts = a writing portfolio; a journal is a less stressful alternative.ملف إنجاز / يوميات
مصطلحات القسم (4)
Portfolio
A purposeful collection of a student's work over time showing progress.ملف إنجاز
Journal writing
Regular personal writing; low-stress practice of fluency.يوميات
Correction codes
Symbols (sp, gr, ww) marking error types.رموز التصحيح
Writing to learn
Using writing to represent and deepen understanding.الكتابة للتعلم

The written corrective feedback debateجدل التصحيح الكتابي

  • Truscott (1996): grammar correction is ineffective; Ferris (1999): clear, selective feedback helps.تروسكوت ضد، وفيريس مع
  • Direct gives the correct form; indirect only marks it (underline, code).مباشر / غير مباشر
  • Focused feedback (one or a few error types) is better supported than unfocused feedback.المركّز أنفع
  • Early drafts: content and organisation; later drafts: language accuracy; learners must act on the feedback.المحتوى أولاً ثم اللغة
مصطلحات القسم (5)
Written corrective feedback (WCF)
Teacher or peer responses to errors in learners' writing.التصحيح الكتابي
Direct corrective feedback
Providing the correct form above or near the error.تصحيح مباشر
Indirect corrective feedback
Indicating an error (underlining, codes) without giving the correction.تصحيح غير مباشر
Focused feedback
Correcting only selected error types.تصحيح مركّز
Unfocused feedback
Correcting all or most errors in a text.تصحيح شامل

Current trends in teaching writingاتجاهات حديثة في تدريس الكتابة

  • Process-genre approach (Badger & White): genre knowledge + writing stages.يجمع النوع والعملية
  • Teaching-learning cycle: context → modelling and deconstructing → joint construction → independent construction.نموذج ← مشتركة ← مستقلة
  • Peer review needs a checklist; a writing conference is a one-to-one talk about a draft.الأقران والمناقشة الفردية
  • Automated writing evaluation (AWE) helps with language; teachers guide content and academic integrity.مساعدة لا بديل
مصطلحات القسم (6)
Process-genre approach
A writing approach combining genre knowledge with process stages.منهج العملية والجنس
Teaching-learning cycle
Genre-based sequence: context, modelling, joint construction, independent construction.دورة التدريس والتعلم
Joint construction
Teacher and learners compose a text together before independent writing.الكتابة المشتركة
Peer review
Learners read and comment on each other's drafts.مراجعة الأقران
Writing conference
A short one-to-one discussion between teacher and student about a piece of writing.مناقشة فردية حول المسودة
Automated writing evaluation (AWE)
Software that scores or gives feedback on writing automatically.التقييم الآلي للكتابة

Text types and Arab writersأنواع النصوص والكاتب العربي

  • The text type sets tense, vocabulary and register; freewriting and outlining are pre-writing.نوع النص يحدد الزمن والرسمية
  • Contrastive rhetoric (Kaplan): Arabic links ideas with 'wa'; Arab writers struggle with cohesion and organisation.الصعوبة في التماسك
  • Fewer full stops in Arabic → English run-on sentences; teach punctuation.علامات الترقيم
مصطلحات القسم (1)
Contrastive rhetoric
The study of how the organisation of writing differs across languages and cultures (Kaplan).البلاغة التقابلية

Matching writing tasks to level and purposeمواءمة مهام الكتابة للمستوى والهدف

  • A complaint letter suits intermediate and advanced learners.للمتوسط والمتقدم
  • Reproduction task: write down content seen, heard or read, as it was.إعادة إنتاج المحتوى
مصطلحات القسم (2)
Transition words
Linking words such as moreover, hence, in addition that connect ideas smoothly.كلمات الانتقال والربط
Reproduction task
A writing task in which learners record content they have seen or heard rather than create or evaluate it.مهمة إعادة الإنتاج
