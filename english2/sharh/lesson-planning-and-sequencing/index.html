شرح تخطيط الدرس وتسلسل الأنشطة (Lesson Planning & Sequencing)

التصميم التعليميالمعيار 6.9.2التخصصي English 2 1448

Know lesson plan components and stages, sequencing principles, curriculum/syllabus types and needs analysis.مكونات الخطة والتسلسل والمنهج

Lesson plan componentsمكونات الخطة

  • Written plans: a guide, a record, evidence for observers — but they do NOT show students' motivation and performance.لا تُظهر الدافعية
  • Information from students' reactions to a lesson = feedback.تغذية راجعة
  • Least important when planning materials: class control (vs objectives, learning styles, cultural background).ضبط الصف الأقل
  • Before planning, consider students' educational and cultural background; first ask 'What will I achieve?'.خلفية الطلاب والهدف
مصطلحات القسم (4)
Lesson plan
A written outline of objectives, procedures, materials, timing and assessment.خطة الدرس
Procedures
The tasks and activities for each stage of a lesson.الإجراءات
Interaction patterns
T–S, S–S, pair and group work arrangements.أنماط التفاعل
Skeleton plan
An outline plan containing general information.خطة هيكل

Lesson stagesمراحل الدرس

  • Warm-up / lead-in: introduces the topic and activates background knowledge.التهيئة
  • Effective order: presentation → practice → production; presentation comes first.العرض أولاً
  • Correct order: lead-in → activity → feedback; the time for each activity is estimated.تهيئة، نشاط، تغذية
  • Consolidation: reviewing and reinforcing new material, usually at the end.التثبيت
  • The teacher moving around, listening and taking notes = monitoring.المتابعة
مصطلحات القسم (4)
Warm-up / Lead-in
Opening stage that introduces the topic and activates prior knowledge.التهيئة
Consolidation
A stage that reviews and reinforces learning.التثبيت
Wrap-up / Closure
Summarizing the lesson and checking objectives.الختام
Monitoring
Observing students as they work to support and assess them.المتابعة

Sequencing principlesمبادئ التسلسل

  • Simple → complex, known → unknown, receptive → productive, controlled → free.مبادئ التدرج
  • Giving an overview before details = whole-to-part sequencing.من الكل للجزء
  • Gradual release: I do → we do → you do; scaffolding is temporary support removed gradually (ZPD).نقل المسؤولية والسقالات
  • Backward design: desired results → evidence → learning plan.التصميم العكسي
  • Activities should be varied, flexible and coherent.متنوعة ومرنة
مصطلحات القسم (4)
Whole-to-part sequencing
Presenting an overview first, then details.من الكل للجزء
Gradual release of responsibility
I do, we do, you do.نقل المسؤولية التدريجي
Scaffolding
Temporary support withdrawn as learners become independent.سقالات
Backward design
Planning from desired outcomes back to activities (Wiggins & McTighe).التصميم العكسي

Curriculum, syllabus and needs analysisالمنهج والمقرر وتحليل الاحتياجات

  • A curriculum is broader (goals, content, methods, evaluation); a syllabus is the content and its sequence.منهج أوسع من مقرر
  • Selecting and organizing content = syllabus design; a curriculum built on predetermined outcomes is product-oriented.تصميم المقرر / موجّه بالمنتج
  • To learn students' interests and abilities, conduct a needs analysis (design, conduct, analyze).تحليل الاحتياجات
مصطلحات القسم (6)
Curriculum
The overall plan: goals, content, methods and evaluation.منهج
Syllabus
The content of a course and its sequence.مقرر
Syllabus design
Selecting and organizing instructional content.تصميم المقرر
Structural syllabus
A syllabus organized by grammar structures.مقرر بنيوي
Product-oriented curriculum
Organized around predetermined learner outcomes.موجّه بالمنتج
Needs analysis
Collecting information about learners' necessities, lacks and wants.تحليل الاحتياجات

Classroom managementإدارة الصف

  • Deciding how closely to work with students = proximity.القرب
  • Wait time after questions (3–5 seconds) increases participation.وقت الانتظار
  • Harmer's teacher roles: controller, organizer, assessor, prompter, participant, resource, tutor, observer.أدوار المعلم
مصطلحات القسم (2)
Proximity
The physical distance between teacher and students.القرب
Wait time
The pause after asking a question before calling on a student.وقت الانتظار

Planning at unit and course levelالتخطيط على مستوى الوحدة والفصل

  • Scheme of work (term/year) → unit plan (lessons linked to unit objectives) → lesson plans.خطة سنوية ← وحدة ← درس
  • Units are sequenced so language is recycled and builds towards a final task or project.الدروس تبني نحو مهمة ختامية
  • Unit-level backward design: results and essential questions → evidence → activities.النتائج ← الأدلة ← الأنشطة
  • Spiral curriculum (Bruner): key content revisited at increasing complexity.مراجعة المحتوى بعمق متزايد
  • Good plans include a contingency plan (Plan B) and homework linked to the objectives.خطة بديلة وواجب مرتبط بالهدف
مصطلحات القسم (4)
Scheme of work
A long-term plan distributing syllabus content across a term or year.توزيع المنهج
Unit plan
A plan for a group of related lessons with shared objectives and a final assessment.خطة الوحدة
Spiral curriculum
A curriculum that revisits topics repeatedly at higher levels of complexity (Bruner).المنهج الحلزوني
Contingency plan
An alternative activity prepared in case the main plan does not work.خطة بديلة

Types of syllabusأنواع المناهج (syllabus)

  • Each syllabus has a unit of organisation: grammar, notions/functions, situations, topics, skills, tasks, words or content.ما وحدة التنظيم؟
  • Wilkins: synthetic = language piece by piece (structural); analytic = whole purposeful chunks (task-based).تركيبي قطعة قطعة / تحليلي كلي
  • Most modern coursebooks use a multi-strand (integrated) syllabus.الكتب الحديثة متعددة الخيوط
مصطلحات القسم (8)
Situational syllabus
A syllabus organised around situations where language is used.منهج المواقف
Topic-based syllabus
A syllabus organised around topics or themes.منهج الموضوعات
Skills-based syllabus
A syllabus organised around language skills and sub-skills.منهج المهارات
Task-based syllabus
A syllabus organised around tasks learners carry out.منهج المهام
Lexical syllabus
A syllabus organised around words, collocations and chunks.منهج معجمي
Synthetic syllabus
Language taught as separate items that learners gradually combine (Wilkins).منهج تركيبي
Analytic syllabus
Language presented in whole meaningful chunks organised by purpose (Wilkins).منهج تحليلي
Multi-strand syllabus
A syllabus integrating several organising strands.منهج متعدد الخيوط

Lesson planning modelsنماذج تخطيط الدروس

  • Whatever the model, an integrated-skills lesson links a receptive skill (reading a text) to a productive one (discussing or writing).ربط الاستقبال بالإنتاج
مصطلحات القسم (3)
Gagné's nine events of instruction
A nine-step instructional sequence from gaining attention to transfer.أحداث التعليم التسعة
Anticipatory set
Hunter's opening step that focuses attention and connects to prior learning.التهيئة
5E model
Engage, Explore, Explain, Elaborate, Evaluate.النموذج الخماسي

Sharing the syllabus and the teaching cycleمشاركة المنهج ودورة التدريس

  • Distributing the syllabus at the start shows content, schedule and expected learning outcomes.توزيع المنهج يوضح النواتج المتوقعة
  • Teaching cycle: preparation → implementation → reflection (judge what worked, then modify).التحضير ← التنفيذ ← التأمل

Classroom management: seating, instructions and routinesإدارة الصف: الجلوس والتعليمات والروتين

  • Seating: rows for teacher-led input and tests; clusters for group projects; horseshoe / circle for discussion; movable furniture for quick changes.كل ترتيب لغرض
  • Give instructions before students move or get materials; then check with ICQs ('Alone or with a partner?').التعليمات قبل الحركة ثم ICQ
  • CCQs check meaning, because 'Do you understand?' usually gets 'yes' without real understanding.سبب استخدام CCQ
  • Minor misbehaviour: non-verbal cues — eye contact, gestures, proximity control — so the lesson continues.إشارات غير لفظية
  • Keep TTT low to raise STT; use clear rules, routines and smooth transitions.قلل كلام المعلم
  • A lesson plan is a flexible roadmap; its assessment part states how objectives will be checked.الخطة خارطة مرنة
مصطلحات القسم (4)
Teacher Talking Time (TTT)
The proportion of lesson time in which the teacher talks.وقت كلام المعلم
Student Talking Time (STT)
The proportion of lesson time in which learners use the language.وقت كلام الطلاب
Horseshoe (U-shape)
Desks arranged in a U so all learners can see each other and the teacher.ترتيب حرف U
Signposting
Language that marks the move from one lesson stage to the next.عبارات الانتقال

أشهر الفخاخ في هذا الدرس

  • Written lesson plans do NOT show students' motivation and performance.لا تُظهر الدافعية
  • Prior knowledge is activated in the warm-up, not production.التهيئة
  • A skeleton plan contains general (not detailed) information.عامة
