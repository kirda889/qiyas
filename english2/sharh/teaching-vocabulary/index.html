شرح تدريس المفردات (Teaching Vocabulary)

أصول تدريس اللغةالمعيار 6.9.1التخصصي English 2 1448

Know types of vocabulary, ways of presenting meaning, vocabulary activities and what makes words memorable.أنواع المفردات وعرض المعنى والأنشطة

Receptive vs productive vocabularyاستقبالية وإنتاجية

  • Words a learner understands when reading or listening = receptive vocabulary; words he can use = productive.يفهمها / يستخدمها
  • Nation: knowing a word = its form, meaning and use.شكل ومعنى واستخدام
مصطلحات القسم (3)
Receptive vocabulary
Words a learner recognizes and understands in listening or reading.مفردات استقبالية
Productive vocabulary
Words a learner can use in speaking and writing.مفردات إنتاجية
Form – meaning – use
Nation's three aspects of knowing a word.شكل ومعنى واستخدام

Presenting meaningعرض المعنى

  • Real objects to show meaning = realia.الأشياء الحقيقية
  • The easiest way to present 'snake' is drawing it on the whiteboard.snake = ارسمها
  • Don't teach words in isolation: context clarifies meaning.لا كلمة بلا سياق
  • The most effective strategy for new words while reading is guessing from context.التخمين من السياق
مصطلحات القسم (4)
Realia
Real objects brought into class to illustrate meaning.أشياء حقيقية تُحضر للفصل
Flashcard
A card with a prompt on one side and the answer/information on the other.بطاقة تعليمية
Semantic map
A diagram linking a word to related words and ideas.خريطة دلالية
Guessing from context
Inferring an unknown word's meaning from surrounding words and clues.التخمين من السياق

Remembering wordsتذكّر الكلمات

  • A word is remembered faster the more frequently it is met (recycling, spaced repetition).التكرار يثبّت الكلمة
  • Incidental learning comes from reading/listening; intentional learning from deliberate study.عرضي / مقصود
مصطلحات القسم (3)
Spaced repetition
Reviewing items at increasing intervals over time.تكرار متباعد
Recycling
Re-using vocabulary in later activities and contexts.إعادة تدوير
Incidental learning
Learning words unintentionally while focusing on meaning.تعلم عرضي

Vocabulary activities and assessmentأنشطة وتقويم المفردات

  • Odd one out, crosswords, matching, bingo = vocabulary activities; crosswords build lexical knowledge.الشاذة والمتقاطعة = مفردات
  • Teaching affixes increases both vocabulary and syntactic ability.السوابق واللواحق
  • Assessment checks meaning, pronunciation, spelling, part of speech and usage.ماذا نقيس في المفردات
مصطلحات القسم (3)
Odd one out
Learners identify the word that does not belong to a group.الكلمة الشاذة
Lexical knowledge
Knowledge of words and their meanings.معرفة معجمية
Affixes
Prefixes and suffixes added to roots.سوابق ولواحق

Collocations, chunks and idiomsالتلازم والكتل والتعابير

  • Words that habitually go together (make a decision) = collocations.تلازم لفظي
  • Michael Lewis's Lexical Approach: language is grammaticalized lexis made of chunks.المدخل المعجمي
  • Idioms: the best vocabulary route to a language's culture.التعابير والثقافة
مصطلحات القسم (4)
Collocation
Words that habitually co-occur (heavy rain, make a mistake).تلازم لفظي
Lexical chunks
Multi-word units learned and used as wholes.كتل لغوية
Lexical Approach
Michael Lewis's approach centred on chunks rather than grammar rules.المدخل المعجمي
Idiom
An expression whose meaning cannot be deduced from its individual words.تعبير اصطلاحي

Current trends: Nation, frequency and corporaاتجاهات حديثة: نيشن والتكرار والمدونات

  • Lexical coverage: readers need about 95% known words for adequate comprehension, about 98% for comfortable, unassisted reading.95% حد أدنى و98% قراءة مريحة
  • Frequent words first: West's General Service List (about 2,000); Coxhead's Academic Word List (570 families).الأكثر تكراراً أولاً
  • Breadth = how many words; depth = how well (spelling, pronunciation, collocations, register, word family).الاتساع كم والعمق كيف
  • Word cards with retrieval and spaced review = efficient deliberate learning.بطاقات مع الاسترجاع
  • A concordance shows a word in real contexts; in data-driven learning learners discover patterns.الكونكوردانس
مصطلحات القسم (7)
Four strands (Nation)
Meaning-focused input, meaning-focused output, language-focused learning and fluency development.خيوط نيشن الأربعة
Lexical coverage
The percentage of running words in a text that a reader knows.نسبة التغطية المعجمية
Word family
A base word with its inflected and derived forms (use, uses, used, useful, useless).عائلة الكلمة
Academic Word List (AWL)
Coxhead's list of 570 word families frequent across academic texts.قائمة الكلمات الأكاديمية
Vocabulary breadth / depth
Size of vocabulary vs quality of knowledge of each word.اتساع / عمق المفردات
Concordance
A list of corpus lines showing a word in its contexts (key word in context).قائمة سياقات الكلمة
Data-driven learning
Learners examine corpus data to discover language patterns themselves (Johns).التعلم من البيانات

Word association and review routinesترابط الكلمات وروتين المراجعة

  • Words linked around one topic (tree → apples, leaves) = association, not synonymy.كلمات مترابطة
  • Review: write, look, practise, repeat over days.اكتب، انظر، تدرّب، كرّر
مصطلحات القسم (1)
Word association
The mental links between words that belong to the same topic or experience (desert – sand – camel).تداعي الكلمات وترابطها

Lexical sets and checking word meaningالمجموعات المعجمية والتحقق من المعنى

  • A lexical set: words from one topic taught together (sports: archery, fencing, rowing, scuba diving).كلمات موضوع واحد معاً
  • Either/or CCQs: 'starving' → 'A little hungry or extremely hungry?'أسئلة ثنائية
مصطلحات القسم (1)
Lexical set
A group of words from the same topic or semantic field presented together.مجموعة معجمية

أشهر الفخاخ في هذا الدرس

  • Odd one out = vocabulary, not listening or reading.الشاذة = مفردات
  • Easiest way to present 'snake' = drawing (official handbook).الرسم
  • Realia = real objects; pictures/drawings are different.الأشياء الحقيقية، لا الصور ولا الرسوم
