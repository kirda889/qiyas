شرح تدريس الاستماع (Teaching Listening)

أصول تدريس اللغةالمعيار 6.9.1التخصصي English 2 1448

Distinguish top-down from bottom-up processing, and know the stages, types and strategies of listening.تفرّق بين المعالجتين وتعرف مراحل الاستماع وأنواعه واستراتيجياته

Receptive vs productive skillsمهارات الاستقبال والإنتاج

  • Listening and reading = receptive skills (input); speaking and writing = productive skills (output).الاستماع والقراءة استقبال
  • Listening is the least understood skill: learners cannot control the speaker's speed.لا يتحكم الطالب في السرعة
  • Input must be meaningful and comprehensible.المدخل ذو معنى ومفهوم
مصطلحات القسم (4)
Receptive skills
Skills in which learners receive and understand language: listening and reading.استقبال: استماع وقراءة
Productive skills
Skills in which learners produce language: speaking and writing.إنتاج: كلام وكتابة
Hearing
The physical act of receiving sound without necessarily interpreting it.سماع الصوت فقط
Listening
An active process of receiving, attending to, understanding and interpreting spoken messages.استماع: فهم وتفسير

Top-down vs bottom-up processingمن الأعلى ومن الأسفل

  • Top-down: background knowledge and context — main idea, predicting, inferring, summarizing.من الأعلى: الخلفية والتوقع
  • Bottom-up: smallest units — sounds, word boundaries, morphemes, discourse markers, grammatical cues.من الأسفل: الأصوات والأدوات
  • The interactive model combines both.التفاعلي يجمعهما
مصطلحات القسم (4)
Top-down processing
Using prior knowledge, context and expectations to understand the overall meaning of a text.من الأعلى: خلفية وسياق
Bottom-up processing
Building meaning from the smallest units — sounds, words and grammar — upward.من الأسفل: من الوحدات الصغرى
Schema (pl. schemata)
The background knowledge structures a learner brings to a text.المخطط الذهني
Interactive model
A model in which top-down and bottom-up processing work together.يدمج المعالجتين

Intensive vs extensive listeningمكثف وموسّع

  • Intensive listening: short, focused work on details and form; develops bottom-up processing.مكثف: قصير ومركّز
  • Extensive listening: long passages for general meaning or pleasure; podcasts are the resource most linked to listening.موسّع: طويل للمتعة كالبودكاست
مصطلحات القسم (2)
Intensive listening
Short, focused listening on specific language features, sounds or details.استماع مكثف
Extensive listening
Listening to long texts for general meaning or enjoyment.استماع موسّع

Stages of a listening lessonمراحل درس الاستماع

  • Before listening: set a listening objective, activate schema, pre-teach key words.قبل: حدد الهدف ونشّط الخلفية
  • First listen = gist; second = specific information; post = discussion or follow-up task.العامة ثم التفاصيل
  • Pausing and asking what comes next trains predicting content.إيقاف المقطع = توقع
  • Listen to a paragraph, then summarize it = a semi-guided task.استمع ولخّص = شبه موجهة
مصطلحات القسم (4)
Pre-listening
Stage that prepares learners: setting a purpose, activating schema, predicting.قبل الاستماع
Listening for gist
Listening to understand the general idea, not every word.الفكرة العامة
Listening for specific information
Listening to find particular details such as names, numbers or dates.معلومة محددة
Signposts
Discourse markers that guide listeners through a talk (firstly, to sum up).علامات الطريق

Types of listening by purposeأنواع الاستماع حسب الغرض

  • Feel-good story as a reward → appreciative; analyzing a message before accepting it → critical.للمتعة تقديري، والحكم نقدي
  • Long speeches: teach selective listening — note main ideas and time references.انتقائي: دوّن الأفكار والأوقات
  • Radio, TV, movies = non-interactive; phone calls and face-to-face talk = interactive.الراديو غير تفاعلي
مصطلحات القسم (6)
Appreciative listening
Listening for enjoyment, e.g. music or stories.تقديري: للمتعة
Comprehensive listening
Listening to understand the message (lectures, directions).استيعابي
Critical listening
Listening to evaluate and judge a message.نقدي
Therapeutic (empathic) listening
Listening to support the speaker and show empathy.علاجي أو تعاطفي
Discriminative listening
Listening to distinguish between different sounds.تمييزي
Selective listening
Listening only for specific information and ignoring the rest.انتقائي

Listener strategies and difficultiesالاستراتيجيات والصعوبات

  • Going on despite unclear parts = tolerance of ambiguity; inferring missing information from context = compensation.تحمّل الغموض / التعويض
  • Cornell method = note-taking; repeating with the speaker simultaneously = shadowing.كورنيل للتدوين، والمحاكاة المتزامنة
  • Brown's factors of difficulty include reduced forms, redundancy, performance variables and rate of delivery.عوامل الصعوبة عند براون
مصطلحات القسم (10)
Tolerance of ambiguity
Accepting that some parts of the input are unclear without stopping.تحمّل الغموض
Compensation strategy
Using context and clues to make up for missing understanding.التعويض
Self-monitoring
Checking one's own comprehension while listening (metacognitive).مراقبة الفهم
Cornell method
A note-taking system with a cue column, notes area and summary.طريقة كورنيل
Shadowing
Repeating speech simultaneously as it is heard.المحاكاة
Dictogloss
Students hear a short text, take notes, then reconstruct it together.إعادة بناء النص
Reduced forms
Contracted spoken forms such as 'gonna', 'wanna'.صيغ مختصرة
Performance variables
Hesitations, false starts and fillers in natural speech.متغيرات الأداء
Redundancy
Repetitions and rephrasing typical of spoken language.التكرار والحشو
Rate of delivery
The speed at which a speaker talks.سرعة الكلام

Current trends: teaching (not testing) listeningاتجاهات حديثة: نعلّم الاستماع لا نختبره

  • Field: the comprehension approach (play, then ask questions) tests listening; a process approach trains sub-skills like word boundaries.نعلّم عمليات الفهم لا نختبرها
  • Metacognitive instruction (Vandergrift): plan/predict → monitor → evaluate/reflect.تخطيط ← مراقبة ← تقويم
  • Short bottom-up work after a meaning-focused listen (dictating chunks, weak forms) helps with fast speech.تدريب على الكلام المتصل
مصطلحات القسم (3)
Comprehension approach
A listening lesson that relies on playing a recording and checking answers to questions, which tests rather than teaches listening (criticised by Field).تشغيل ثم أسئلة: اختبار لا تعليم
Metacognitive instruction
Teaching learners to plan, monitor and evaluate their own listening so they become aware of how they listen.تعليم التخطيط والمراقبة والتقويم
Planning / monitoring / evaluating
The three core metacognitive strategies: preparing for a task, checking comprehension during it, and judging success afterwards.الاستراتيجيات ما وراء المعرفية الثلاث

Bottom-up listening and linguistic knowledgeالاستماع من الأسفل والمعرفة اللغوية

  • Bottom-up relies on grammatical (linguistic) competence; top-down on background knowledge.من الأسفل = معرفة لغوية
  • Following spoken classroom instructions is itself a listening task.اتباع التعليمات استماع

Choosing listening texts and pre-listening tasksاختيار نص الاستماع ومهام ما قبله

  • Learner interest in the topic is the factor most linked to an engaging listening text.الموضوع الممتع أهم عامل
  • Pre-listening tasks: predicting from a title or picture, generating questions, pre-teaching words.قبل: توقع وتوليد أسئلة
مصطلحات القسم (1)
Question generation
A pre-listening task in which learners write questions they expect the text to answer, giving them a purpose for listening.توليد أسئلة قبل الاستماع

أشهر الفخاخ في هذا الدرس

  • Recognizing words = bottom-up; listening for the main idea = top-down.لا تخلط بينهما
  • Intensive listening develops bottom-up processing; extensive = pleasure / general meaning.المكثف من الأسفل
  • 'Listen and do' activities = Total Physical Response (TPR), not a listening type.اسمع ونفّذ = TPR
  • Before listening: set a listening objective (not 'verify predictions').الهدف قبل الاستماع

يتابعون فهم الأحداث ويستمتعون.
