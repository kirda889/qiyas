شرح طرق التدريس 2: التواصلية والحديثة (Methods II: Communicative & Modern Strategies)

أصول تدريس اللغةالمعيار 6.9.4التخصصي English 2 1448

Know CLT, TBLT, CBI/CLIL, cooperative learning, eclecticism and strategies for engagement and critical thinking.التواصلي والحديث

Communicative Language Teaching (CLT)التدريس التواصلي

  • CLT = linguistic competence plus the ability to use language appropriately (communicative competence — Hymes).قواعد + استخدام مناسب (هايمز)
  • Meaning first; the current dominant approach in TESOL.السائد اليوم
  • Suits extroverted students; the teacher is a facilitator.يناسب المنبسطين
مصطلحات القسم (3)
Communicative Language Teaching
An approach aiming at communicative competence through meaningful interaction.التدريس التواصلي
Facilitator
A teacher who guides and supports learning rather than lecturing.ميسّر
Notional-functional syllabus
A syllabus organized by notions and functions (Wilkins).منهج المفاهيم والوظائف

Task-based and content-based teachingالمهمة والمحتوى

  • TBLT emerged from the communicative approach (Prabhu's Bangalore project); Willis: pre-task → task cycle → language focus.نشأ من التواصلي (برابو)، وإطار ويليس
  • Projects producing posters, brochures and pamphlets = task-based learning.الملصقات = بالمهمة
  • Small groups solving real-world problems = problem-based learning.حل المشكلات
  • Content-based instruction uses subject matter; family/work/weather topics = theme-based model.المحتوى / الموضوعي
  • Task-, theme- and content-based = integrative teaching; competency-based = what learners can do.تكاملي / الكفايات
مصطلحات القسم (5)
Task-Based Language Teaching (TBLT)
Learning through meaningful tasks with real-world outcomes.التعلم بالمهمة
Problem-based learning
Groups acquire and apply knowledge to solve real-world problems.التعلم بحل المشكلات
Content-Based Instruction (CBI)
Teaching language through subject-matter content.القائم على المحتوى
CLIL
Content and Language Integrated Learning: content, communication, cognition, culture.دمج المحتوى واللغة
Competency-based language teaching
Teaching organized around what learners can do in the language.تدريس الكفايات

Cooperative learning and engagementالتعلم التعاوني والاندماج

  • Cooperative learning builds critical thinking and problem-solving and interpersonal skills; it enhances negotiation of meaning.تعاوني
  • Project work is student-centred (NOT teacher-centred).المشروع للطالب
  • Engagement rises with learning by doing, more wait time and less teacher talk.التعلم بالعمل
  • Think-Pair-Share promotes discussion; pair work fosters respect and friendship.فكّر وزاوج وشارك
  • Anxiety is reduced when teachers act as partners and mentors.شريك ومرشد
مصطلحات القسم (4)
Cooperative learning
Small-group learning with positive interdependence and individual accountability.تعلم تعاوني
Think-Pair-Share
Think alone, discuss with a partner, share with the class.فكّر، زاوج، شارك
Learner-centred instruction
Students are the main focus; the teacher acts as a guide.متمحور حول الطالب
Two Stars and a Wish
Peer feedback: two strengths and one improvement.نجمتان وأمنية

Modern approaches and trendsمداخل واتجاهات حديثة

  • Flipped classroom: material studied before class; class time for discussion.الصف المقلوب
  • Eclectic method: choosing the most suitable techniques from different methods.الانتقائية: الأنسب
  • A rising trend in TESOL: the gamification of lessons.التلعيب
  • Biggest shift: from the teacher to the student; the ideal teacher has linguistic and intercultural competence.من المعلم للطالب
  • Socratic method = teaching through questions; symposium = discussion; inquiry = question and answer.سقراطية / ندوة
مصطلحات القسم (6)
Flipped classroom
Content is studied at home; class time is used for practice and discussion.الصف المقلوب
Eclecticism
Selecting the most suitable techniques from various methods.الانتقائية
Post-method pedagogy
Kumaravadivelu: particularity, practicality, possibility.ما بعد الطريقة
Gamification
Using game elements to increase engagement.التلعيب
Dogme ELT
Materials-light, conversation-driven teaching (Thornbury).تدريس بلا كتاب
Facilitator teaching style
Grasha's style that encourages self-directed learning.أسلوب الميسّر

Critical thinkingالتفكير الناقد

  • Critical thinking = students evaluate propositions — not memorizing, translating or copying.تقييم الطروحات
  • 'What is an alternative conclusion?' builds higher-order thinking, as do topics like 'crisis management'.أسئلة عليا
  • Teaching that relates to students' feelings and beliefs is more effective.ربط بالمشاعر
مصطلحات القسم (2)
Critical thinking
Analyzing and evaluating ideas and arguments to form judgments.تفكير ناقد
Higher-order thinking skills (HOTS)
Analysis, evaluation and creation.مهارات عليا

The functional approachالمنهج الوظيفي

  • The functional (notional-functional) approach organises teaching around functions — requesting, apologising, inviting — not grammar forms.الوظائف قبل القواعد
مصطلحات القسم (1)
Functional approach
Teaching organised around communicative functions such as requesting or apologising.تدريس قائم على الوظائف

أشهر الفخاخ في هذا الدرس
