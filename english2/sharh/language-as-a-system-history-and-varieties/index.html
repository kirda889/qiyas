What is language? What is linguistics?اللغة واللسانيات

  • Language = an arbitrary system of vocal symbols for communication; its scientific study = linguistics.نظام رموز اعتباطية / اللسانيات
  • 'Don't split infinitives' = a prescriptive rule; descriptive grammar describes actual use.معياري / وصفي
  • Least accurate description: 'language is only verbal' — it can also be written or signed.ليست لفظية فقط

Descriptive grammar
A description of how people actually use language.قواعد وصفية
Langue / Parole

Applied linguistics

Historical linguistics

Neurolinguistics

Design features (Hockett)خصائص اللغة البشرية

  • The relationship between form and meaning is arbitrary (Hockett).اعتباطية
  • Referring to things not present = displacement.الإزاحة
  • Endless new messages = creativity / productivity; meaningless sounds → meaningful units = duality.الإبداعية / ازدواجية البنية
  • Main problem of origin-of-language theories: they lack scientific evidence.بلا دليل علمي
  • Variation is an inherent part of language; all languages change (mutability).التنوع أصيل والتغيّر
مصطلحات القسم (7)
Arbitrariness
No natural connection between a word's form and its meaning.اعتباطية
Displacement
The ability to refer to things not present in time or space.الإزاحة
Productivity (creativity)
The ability to produce and understand infinite new utterances.الإبداعية
Duality of patterning
Language works at two levels: sounds and meaningful units.ازدواجية البنية
Cultural transmission
Language is learned from the community, not inherited.النقل الثقافي
Discreteness
Language is made of distinct units.التمايز
Mutability
The grammars of all languages change over time.قابلية التغيّر

Functions of languageوظائف اللغة

  • Passing on information = transactional function; expressing social relations = interactional.تبادلية / تفاعلية
  • 'Nice weather, isn't it?' = phatic communication; functionalists: language developed to communicate.اتصالية
مصطلحات القسم (4)
Transactional function
Language used to convey information/content.تبادلية
Interactional function
Language used to establish and maintain social relationships.تفاعلية
Phatic communication
Language used for social contact rather than information.اتصالية
Utterance
A spoken word or sentence; a unit of verbal communication.ملفوظ

History and sources of Englishتاريخ الإنجليزية ومصادر كلماتها

  • English is Germanic (Indo-European); Spanish, Portuguese, Italian = Romance; Arabic = Semitic.جرمانية
  • Old English → Middle English (after the Norman Conquest 1066) → Early Modern (Shakespeare, printing) → Modern.مراحل الإنجليزية
  • café → French; scientific terms → Greek and Latin; alcohol, algebra, sugar → Arabic (borrowing).مصادر الكلمات
  • Everyday words (the, be, have) are Germanic; most dictionary words come from Latin/French.الأكثر استخداماً جرمانية
مصطلحات القسم (5)
Indo-European
The language family that includes the Germanic and Romance branches.هندو-أوروبية
Germanic
The branch of Indo-European that includes English, German and Dutch.جرمانية
Romance languages
Languages descended from Latin: Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, French.رومانسية
Norman Conquest (1066)
Event that brought many French words into English.الغزو النورماندي
Great Vowel Shift
A major change in English long-vowel pronunciation (1400–1700).التحول الصوتي الكبير

Varieties of languageتنوعات اللغة

  • Dialect = grammar, vocabulary and pronunciation; accent = pronunciation only.لهجة / لكنة
  • Simplified contact language with no native speakers = pidgin; a pidgin that became a first language = creole.هجين / كريول
  • ELF focuses on intelligibility; Saudi Arabia is in Kachru's expanding circle.الوضوح / الدائرة المتسعة
  • A map line separating areas with different language features = isogloss.خط تساوي الظاهرة
  • British vs American: colour/color (Americans write -or), petrol/gasoline, lift/elevator.بريطاني / أمريكي
مصطلحات القسم (9)
Dialect
A variety differing in grammar, vocabulary and pronunciation.لهجة
Accent
A variety differing only in pronunciation.لكنة
Idiolect
An individual's personal way of speaking.لغة الفرد
Register
Language variety according to situation and formality.مستوى الخطاب
Pidgin
A simplified contact language with no native speakers.هجين
Creole
A pidgin that has become the first language of a community.كريول
Lingua franca
A common language used between speakers of different L1s.لغة مشتركة

Kachru's three circles
Inner (UK, US), outer (India, Nigeria), expanding (Saudi Arabia, China).دوائر كاتشرو

Theories of the origin of language (Yule)نظريات أصل اللغة عند يول

  • Each theory picks one possible source; none can be proven — there is no direct physical evidence of the earliest speech.كلها بلا دليل مباشر
مصطلحات القسم (8)
Divine source
The theory that language was given to humans by God.المصدر الإلهي
Bow-wow theory
Natural sound source: words began as imitations of natural sounds.نظرية محاكاة الأصوات
Onomatopoeia
Words that imitate the sounds associated with what they refer to (buzz, splash).محاكاة صوتية
Pooh-pooh theory
Language began from instinctive emotional cries.نظرية صيحات الانفعال
Yo-heave-ho theory
Language began from sounds made during collective physical work.نظرية العمل الجماعي
Oral-gesture theory
Language developed from a link between hand gestures and mouth movements.نظرية الإيماءة الفموية
Physiological adaptation
The evolution of human speech organs and brain features that make speech possible.التكيف الفسيولوجي
Innateness hypothesis
The genetic-source view that humans are born with a capacity for language.فرضية الفطرية

Saussure and definitions of languageسوسير وتعريفات اللغة

  • Ferdinand de Saussure, founder of modern structural linguistics (Course in General Linguistics, 1916, published after his death).مؤسس اللسانيات البنيوية
  • Classic definitions: Sapir (purely human, non-instinctive symbols), Hall (habitual oral-auditory symbols), Chomsky (a set of sentences).تعريفات سابير وهول وتشومسكي
مصطلحات القسم (7)
Linguistic sign
Saussure's unit combining a signifier (form) and a signified (concept).العلامة اللغوية
Signifier
The form of a sign: its sound-image or written form.الدال
Signified
The concept or meaning a sign refers to.المدلول
Synchronic linguistics
Studying a language as a system at a particular point in time.اللسانيات التزامنية
Diachronic linguistics
Studying how a language changes over time.اللسانيات التعاقبية
Syntagmatic relations
Relations between linguistic units that combine in sequence.علاقات تركيبية
Paradigmatic relations
Relations between units that can substitute for each other in the same position.علاقات استبدالية

More of Hockett's design featuresالمزيد من سمات هوكيت

  • Interchangeability: anyone can send and receive; total feedback: speakers monitor their own messages.تبادل الأدوار والتغذية الراجعة
  • Specialisation: speech serves communication only; semanticity: signals have fixed meanings.التخصص والدلالية
  • Vocal-auditory channel; rapid fading: spoken signals disappear quickly.القناة الصوتية والزوال السريع
  • Later additions: prevarication (lying, imagining), reflexiveness (language about language), learnability.الكذب والانعكاسية وقابلية التعلم
مصطلحات القسم (7)
Interchangeability
Any user of a language can both send and receive messages.التبادلية
Total feedback
Speakers can hear and monitor what they themselves say.التغذية الراجعة الكاملة
Specialisation
Linguistic signals have communication as their only function.التخصص
Semanticity
Linguistic signals have specific, fixed meanings.الدلالية
Rapid fading
Spoken signals are transitory and quickly disappear.الزوال السريع
Prevarication
The ability to say things that are false, misleading or imaginary.القدرة على الكذب
Reflexiveness
The ability to use language to talk about language itself.الانعكاسية

Standard varieties and social variationالأنماط المعيارية والتنوع الاجتماعي

  • Standard English (education, publishing, media) is defined by grammar and vocabulary; any accent can carry it.المعيارية تخص القواعد والمفردات
  • RP = the prestige non-regional British accent; General American (GA) = the broad US standard accent.البريطاني المعياري والأمريكي العام
  • RP is non-rhotic (car /kɑː/); GA is rhotic (car /kɑr/) and often flaps the t in 'water'.نطق الراء بعد الصائت
  • Sociolect = a social group's variety; slang = informal group words; jargon = professional vocabulary; speech community = shared norms.لهجة اجتماعية، عامية، مصطلحات مهنية
مصطلحات القسم (8)
Standard English
The prestige variety of English used in formal writing, education and media.الإنجليزية المعيارية
Received Pronunciation (RP)
The prestige, non-regional accent of British English.النطق المعياري البريطاني
General American (GA)
The broad standard accent of American English.الأمريكي العام
Rhotic accent
An accent in which /r/ is pronounced in all positions, including after vowels.لكنة تنطق الراء
Sociolect
A variety of language used by a particular social group.لهجة اجتماعية
Slang
Very informal, often temporary vocabulary used by a particular group.العامية
Jargon
Specialised technical vocabulary of a profession or field.لغة المهنة
Speech community
A group of people who share a language variety and rules for its use.مجتمع لغوي

More British–American differencesفروق أخرى بين البريطانية والأمريكية

  • Main BrE/AmE differences: vocabulary (lift/elevator), spelling, pronunciation; grammar differs very little.الفرق الأكبر في المفردات
  • BrE uses past forms in -t more (learnt, burnt); AmE prefers -ed (learned, burned).-t بريطانية / -ed أمريكية
  • oxen and children keep the Old English plural ending -en (history).-en من الإنجليزية القديمة

Language as a rule-governed systemاللغة نظام قواعدي

  • Language is systematic: rules and patterns at every level (sounds, words, sentences), not random.اللغة منهجية منظمة

How English spreadكيف انتشرت الإنجليزية

  • Settlers from England carried English to North America, then Australia; both developed their own inner-circle varieties.من إنجلترا إلى أمريكا وأستراليا
