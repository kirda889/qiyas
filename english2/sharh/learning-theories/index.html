شرح نظريات التعلم (Learning Theories)

Behaviorismالسلوكية

  • Learning = imitation and habit formation: stimulus → response → reinforcement; the mind is a blank slate.تقليد وعادات، والعقل صفحة بيضاء
  • B.F. Skinner (operant conditioning) led it; it produced the Audio-Lingual Method and Contrastive Analysis.سكنر ← السمعية الشفهية والتحليل التقابلي
  • Operant: reinforcement follows the response; classical (Pavlov): it is linked to the stimulus.إجرائي / كلاسيكي
مصطلحات القسم (4)
Behaviorism
Learning as habit formation through stimulus, response and reinforcement.السلوكية
Tabula rasa (blank slate)
The behaviorist view that the mind starts empty.صفحة بيضاء
Operant conditioning
Behavior shaped by consequences (reinforcement after a response) — Skinner.إشراط إجرائي
Classical conditioning
Learning by associating stimuli — Pavlov.إشراط كلاسيكي

Nativism (Chomsky)الفطرية

  • Chomsky: humans are born with a Language Acquisition Device (LAD); all languages share Universal Grammar.جهاز الاكتساب والقواعد الكلية
  • Evidence: children say what they never heard ('I goed') — they apply rules, not just imitate.goed دليل على القاعدة لا التقليد
  • Competence = underlying knowledge of language; performance = actual use.كفاية / أداء
  • Lenneberg's Critical Period Hypothesis (age-linked acquisition) supports nativism.المرحلة الحرجة
  • Innatists: input alone stimulates acquisition; feedback does not change the sequence of development.المدخل يكفي
مصطلحات القسم (6)
Nativism (innatism)
The view that humans are born with an innate capacity for language.الفطرية
Language Acquisition Device (LAD)
Chomsky's proposed innate mental faculty for language.جهاز اكتساب اللغة
Universal Grammar
Principles and properties shared by all languages.القواعد الكلية
Competence
A speaker's underlying (subconscious) knowledge of the language system.الكفاية
Performance
The actual production and use of language.الأداء
Critical Period Hypothesis
The ability to acquire language is biologically linked to age.فرضية المرحلة الحرجة

Cognitivism and Piagetالمعرفية وبياجيه

  • Acquisition is rule formation; language development follows cognitive development (attention, memory, linking new to known).تكوين قواعد مرتبط بالنمو المعرفي
  • Piaget: assimilation fits new information into a schema; accommodation changes the schema.تمثّل / مواءمة
  • Piaget's preoperational stage (2–7 years): symbols, imagination, memorization.2–7 سنوات
  • Ausubel: meaningful learning links new material to existing knowledge (advance organizer).التعلم ذو المعنى
مصطلحات القسم (5)
Cognitivism
Learning as active mental processing and rule formation.المعرفية
Accommodation
Modifying a schema to fit new information.مواءمة
Assimilation
Incorporating new information into an existing schema.تمثّل
Schema
An organized mental structure of knowledge.مخطط ذهني
Cognitive development
The emergence of mental abilities and skills that make up the human intellect.النمو المعرفي

Constructivism and Vygotskyالبنائية وفيجوتسكي

  • Learners construct knowledge; Bruner: discovery learning and the spiral curriculum.بناء المعرفة
  • Sociocultural theory (Vygotsky): learning happens with a more knowledgeable other inside the ZPD.التفاعل داخل منطقة النمو القريبة
  • Scaffolding supports learners until they work alone; it makes input more comprehensible.السقالات
  • Interactionist view: meaningful conversation drives acquisition — hence collaborative writing and peer feedback.التفاعل والتعاون
مصطلحات القسم (5)
Zone of Proximal Development (ZPD)
The gap between what a learner can do alone and with help.منطقة النمو القريبة
More Knowledgeable Other (MKO)
A teacher or peer who supports learning.الأكثر معرفة
Scaffolding
Temporary support gradually removed as competence grows.سقالات
Sociocultural theory
Learning as a social process mediated by interaction.النظرية الاجتماعية الثقافية
Discovery learning
Learners find principles for themselves (Bruner).التعلم بالاكتشاف

First language acquisition and views of languageاكتساب اللغة الأولى

  • Child stages: cooing/babbling → one word → two-word pivot grammar → telegraphic speech (no function words).مراحل الطفل
  • Children imitate deep rather than surface structure ('Nobody don't like me').تقليد البنية العميقة
  • Views of language: structural (system), functional (functions), interactional (relationships).بنيوية / وظيفية / تفاعلية
  • Others: Gestalt (whole > parts), humanism (Maslow, Rogers), social learning (Bandura).نظريات أخرى
مصطلحات القسم (4)
Telegraphic speech
Speech using content words without function words ('Daddy go car').كلام برقي
Pivot grammar
Early two-word combinations ('allgone shoe').قواعد المحور
Structural view of language
Language as a system of structurally related elements for transmitting meaning.النظرة البنيوية
Gestalt
The whole is greater than the sum of its parts.الجشطالت

Theories of writingنظريات الكتابة

  • Ecological theory (Cooper): writing is shaped by place, community and local context.البيئية ← المكان والسياق المحلي
  • Cognitive theory: the writer's mental processes (planning, translating, reviewing); sociocultural: collaboration and peer feedback.المعرفية / الاجتماعية الثقافية
مصطلحات القسم (1)
Ecological theory of writing
A view that writing is shaped by the writer's place, community and local context.النظرية البيئية في الكتابة

Generative grammar and innatismالقواعد التوليدية والفطرية

  • Chomsky's generative grammar (rules that produce infinite sentences) is studied under innatism (nativism).التوليدية تابعة للفطرية
مصطلحات القسم (1)
Generative grammar
Chomsky's model of the finite set of rules that can generate all grammatical sentences of a language.القواعد التوليدية

Piaget's four stages and peer scaffoldingمراحل بياجيه الأربع والدعم بين الأقران

  • Social constructivism: a more capable peer scaffolds a weaker one within the ZPD — the basis for mixed-ability pairing.زميل أقدر يدعم الأضعف
مصطلحات القسم (3)
Concrete operational stage
Piaget's stage (about 7–11) when children reason logically about concrete situations.المرحلة الإجرائية المحسوسة
Formal operational stage
Piaget's stage (from about 11) when abstract and hypothetical reasoning develops.المرحلة الإجرائية الشكلية
Social constructivism
The view that knowledge is built through social interaction with more capable others.البنائية الاجتماعية

أشهر الفخاخ في هذا الدرس

  • Changing existing structures = accommodation; fitting in without change = assimilation.مواءمة / تمثّل
  • The Critical Period Hypothesis supports nativism, not behaviorism.الفطرية
  • Blank slate = behaviorism.السلوكية
