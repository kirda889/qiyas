شرح المتعلمون: الأنماط والدافعية والثقافة (Learners: Styles, Differences, Motivation & Culture)

التصميم التعليميالمعيار 6.9.7التخصصي English 2 1448

Know learning styles, multiple intelligences, types of motivation, affective factors and culturally responsive planning.الأنماط والذكاءات والدافعية

Learning stylesأنماط التعلم

  • Remembers names by seeing them = visual; prefers physical activity = kinesthetic (e.g. arranging jumbled word cut-outs).بصري / حركي
  • Problem-solving works best with analytic learners; logical learners make lists and find relationships.تحليلي / منطقي
مصطلحات القسم (5)
Visual learner
Learns best through images and seeing.بصري
Auditory learner
Learns best through listening and discussion.سمعي
Kinesthetic (tactile) learner
Learns best through movement and hands-on activity.حركي
Analytic learner
Prefers step-by-step, logical problem-solving.تحليلي
Kolb's learning styles
Diverging, assimilating, converging, accommodating.أنماط كولب

Multiple intelligences and differentiationالذكاءات والتمايز

  • Gardner's multiple intelligences: word games and crosswords → linguistic; group activities → interpersonal.ذكاءات غاردنر
  • Differentiated instruction adapts content, process, product and environment to individual needs.التعليم المتمايز
  • Design lessons for students' levels and abilities, culture and interests.حسب المستوى والثقافة
مصطلحات القسم (5)
Multiple intelligences
Gardner's theory of several distinct intelligences.الذكاءات المتعددة
Interpersonal intelligence
Ability to understand and work with others.اجتماعي
Intrapersonal intelligence
Ability to understand oneself.ذاتي
Linguistic intelligence
Sensitivity to words and language.لغوي
Differentiated instruction
Tailoring teaching to individual learners' needs (Tomlinson).تعليم متمايز

Motivationالدافعية

  • Motivation is the key factor in success or failure.العامل الأهم
  • Intrinsic: satisfying in itself; extrinsic: external rewards (promotion, grades, academic requirements).داخلية / خارجية
  • Integrative: interest in the target people and culture; instrumental: job, scholarship, passing an exam.تكاملية / نفعية
  • Curiosity motivates from inside; correcting every mistake demotivates.ما يحفّز وما يحبط
  • Dörnyei: motivation grows from the learner's vision of an ideal L2 self.الذات المثالية
مصطلحات القسم (5)
Intrinsic motivation
Doing an activity for its own enjoyment and satisfaction.داخلية
Extrinsic motivation
Doing an activity to gain external rewards.خارجية
Integrative motivation
Desire to integrate with the target-language community and culture.تكاملية
Instrumental motivation
Learning for practical gain such as a job or exam.نفعية
L2 Motivational Self System
Dörnyei: ideal L2 self, ought-to self, learning experience.نظام دورنيي

Affective factors and cultureالعوامل الوجدانية والثقافة

  • Anxiety and self-restriction are affective factors; modeling performance builds confidence.وجداني
  • Teaching greetings and social norms = teaching culture; a multinational class needs cross-cultural teaching.الثقافة
  • Avoid culturally inappropriate texts in a Saudi classroom.الملاءمة الثقافية
مصطلحات القسم (3)
Foreign language anxiety
Fear and tension specific to learning/using a foreign language.قلق اللغة
Willingness to communicate
A learner's readiness to speak when given the chance.الرغبة في التواصل
Cross-cultural teaching
Teaching students from different cultural backgrounds.تدريس عبر الثقافات

Gifted, struggling and mixed-ability classesالموهوبون والمتعثرون والفصول متفاوتة المستوى

  • Mixed-ability classes: tiered tasks (same objective, different support), open-ended tasks, extension tasks for fast finishers.مهام متدرجة ومفتوحة وإضافية
  • Flexible grouping: similar-level groups for targeted help; mixed groups for peer tutoring.تجميع مرن
  • Gifted learners: enrichment, acceleration, curriculum compacting — not 'more of the same'.إثراء وتسريع وضغط المنهج
  • Struggling learners: pre-taught vocabulary, small steps, visuals, extra time, remedial lessons.تبسيط وعلاج
مصطلحات القسم (8)
Mixed-ability class
A class with learners of widely different levels and abilities.فصل متفاوت المستوى
Tiered tasks
Versions of a task at different levels of difficulty for the same objective.مهام متدرجة
Open-ended task
A task with many acceptable answers so learners respond at their own level.مهمة مفتوحة
Extension task
Extra, more challenging work for learners who finish early.مهمة إضافية
Enrichment
Deeper or broader learning experiences for able learners.الإثراء
Curriculum compacting
Skipping content a gifted learner has mastered and using the time for advanced work.ضغط المنهج
Remedial teaching
Extra instruction targeting specific weaknesses found through assessment.التدريس العلاجي
Interest inventory
A questionnaire that collects learners' interests to personalise lessons.استبيان الاهتمامات

Learning difficulties and cognitive stylesصعوبات التعلم والأساليب المعرفية

  • Dyslexia: persistent decoding and spelling difficulty — multisensory techniques, clear fonts, extra time.عسر القراءة: متعدد الحواس
  • ADHD: short varied tasks, clear routines, movement breaks, seating near the teacher.فرط الحركة: مهام قصيرة وروتين
  • Field-independent: details apart from context (analysis); field-dependent: the whole, socially oriented.الاستقلال عن المجال / الاعتماد عليه
  • Reflective learners take time and make fewer errors; impulsive learners answer quickly and guess more.التروي والاندفاع
مصطلحات القسم (4)
Dyslexia
A specific learning difficulty mainly affecting reading accuracy and spelling.عسر القراءة
ADHD
Attention deficit hyperactivity disorder: difficulty with attention, hyperactivity and impulsivity.فرط الحركة وتشتت الانتباه
Field independence / dependence
A cognitive style: perceiving details separately from their context vs perceiving the whole field (Witkin).الاستقلال / الاعتماد على المجال
Reflectivity vs impulsivity
A cognitive style: slow, careful responses vs quick, risk-taking responses.التروي / الاندفاع

Culture and participationالثقافة والمشاركة

  • Students from collectivist cultures are often reluctant to ask questions in class; small groups and anonymous slips help.تجنب السؤال العلني
  • Immediate public criticism discourages participation; praise effort and give wait time.النقد الفوري يقتل المشاركة
مصطلحات القسم (1)
Collectivist culture
A culture that values group harmony and belonging over individual expression.ثقافة جماعية

Personality and nonverbal cultureالشخصية والتواصل غير اللفظي

  • Introverted learners often prefer quiet, individual work such as an independent project; debates suit extroverts.الانطوائي يناسبه العمل الفردي
  • Gestures are culture-specific: nonverbal behaviour is part of teaching culture.الإيماءات تختلف بين الثقافات
مصطلحات القسم (1)
Introversion
A personality trait marked by preferring reflection and individual work to social interaction.الانطوائية

Technology and motivationالتقنية والدافعية

  • Interactive technology (games, quizzes, multimedia) raises engagement and motivation.التقنية التفاعلية ترفع الدافعية

أشهر الفخاخ في هذا الدرس

  • Remembering names by seeing them = visual.بصري
  • Working for a promotion = extrinsic (instrumental).خارجية
  • Group work develops interpersonal (not intrapersonal) skills.اجتماعي
