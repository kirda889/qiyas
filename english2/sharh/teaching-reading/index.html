Skimming and scanningالقراءة السريعة والانتقائية

  • Skimming: quick reading for the gist (choosing a title, overviewing a chapter).سريعة للفكرة العامة
  • Scanning: finding specific information — a date, a name, a dictionary word.انتقائية لمعلومة محددة
مصطلحات القسم (4)
Skimming
Reading quickly to get the general idea or gist of a text.قراءة سريعة
Scanning
Reading quickly to locate specific information.قراءة انتقائية
Reading for gist
Reading to understand the main idea only.الفكرة العامة
Reading for detail
Careful reading to understand specific points.التفاصيل

Intensive and extensive readingمكثفة وموسّعة

  • Extensive reading: lots of easy, enjoyable material (graded readers).كثير وسهل وممتع
  • Intensive reading: a short text studied closely for linguistic and semantic details.نص قصير مفصّل
مصطلحات القسم (3)
Extensive reading
Reading large quantities of easy material for pleasure and fluency.موسّعة
Intensive reading
Close, detailed reading of a short text for language and meaning.مكثفة
Graded readers
Books simplified to suit learners' levels.قصص متدرجة

Reading modelsنماذج القراءة

  • Reading word by word and looking up every unknown word is a bottom-up strategy.كلمة كلمة = من الأسفل
  • Decoding is the first stage in learning to read.فك الترميز أولاً
مصطلحات القسم (5)
Bottom-up reading
Building meaning from letters and words in sequence.من الأسفل
Top-down reading
Using prior knowledge and context to predict and interpret.من الأعلى
Interactive-compensatory model
Readers use both processes and compensate for weaknesses in one with the other.التفاعلي التعويضي
Decoding
Translating written symbols into sounds and words.فك الترميز
Schema activation
Using background knowledge to understand a passage.تنشيط الخلفية

Comprehension strategies and levelsاستراتيجيات ومستويات الفهم

  • Prior knowledge + title before reading = predicting; background knowledge + text clues = inferring.توقع / استنتاج
  • Main ideas in one's own words = summarizing; same idea, different wording = paraphrasing.تلخيص / إعادة صياغة
  • Identifying and fixing problems with understanding = monitoring.مراقبة الفهم
  • 'How plausible is the poor man's story?' after reading = critical reading.تقويم النص = قراءة ناقدة
  • Reading texts by the same author or theme = text-to-text connections.ربط نص بنص
  • Identifying the purpose of reading increases comprehension and retention.تحديد الهدف يرفع الفهم
مصطلحات القسم (10)
Inferring
Using background knowledge and text clues to reach unstated meaning.استنتاج
Predicting
Using clues and prior knowledge to anticipate content.توقع
Summarizing
Stating the main ideas briefly in one's own words.تلخيص
Paraphrasing
Restating the same idea with significantly different wording.إعادة صياغة
Comprehension monitoring
Noticing and repairing breakdowns in understanding while reading.مراقبة الفهم
Literal comprehension
Understanding what is explicitly stated.فهم حرفي
Interpretive comprehension
Using logic to infer meaning beyond the text.فهم تفسيري
Critical reading
Evaluating and judging a text's ideas, purpose and credibility.قراءة ناقدة
Text-to-text connection
Linking a text to another text with a common author or theme.ربط بين نصين
Visualizing
Forming mental images while reading.تصوّر

Cueing systems, early reading and toolsأنظمة التلميح وأدوات القراءة

  • Word from picture and context = semantic cueing; grammar fit = syntactic; letter–sound = graphophonic.دلالي / نحوي / صوتي
  • Rhyming and syllable games build phonological awareness: segmenting (word → sounds), blending (sounds → word).الوعي الصوتي
  • Dyslexia: improve word recognition; fluency begins with direct instruction.عسر القراءة
  • Maps, graphs, pictures = pictorial aids; an English–English dictionary best expands vocabulary.وسائل تصويرية وقاموس أحادي
  • Routines: KWL, SQ3R, reciprocal teaching.روتينات القراءة
مصطلحات القسم (10)
Semantic cueing
Using meaning, context and pictures to identify words.تلميح دلالي
Syntactic cueing
Using sentence structure to identify words.تلميح نحوي
Graphophonic cueing
Using letter–sound relationships.تلميح صوتي كتابي
Phonological awareness
Awareness of the sound structure of words (rhymes, syllables, phonemes).وعي صوتي
Segmenting
Breaking a word into its individual sounds.تجزئة
Blending
Combining individual sounds to form a word.دمج
KWL chart
Know – Want to know – Learned organizer.جدول KWL
SQ3R
Survey, Question, Read, Recite, Review.طريقة SQ3R
Reciprocal teaching
Predict, question, clarify, summarize in turns.التدريس التبادلي
Authentic material
Texts produced for real-world purposes (web pages, newspapers, menus).مادة أصيلة

Current trends: extensive reading, fluency and phonicsاتجاهات حديثة: القراءة الموسعة والطلاقة والصوتيات

  • Day & Bamford: material is easy, varied and chosen by learners; they read a lot, for pleasure, with few tests; the teacher models reading.سهل، يختاره الطالب، كثير، للمتعة
  • Fluency: repeated reading and timed reading (words per minute).المتكررة والمؤقتة
  • Synthetic phonics: sounds, then blend (/k/ /æ/ /t/ → cat); analytic: patterns in known words.تركيبية / تحليلية
  • Arab learners need work on vowel letters and left-to-right word recognition.الحروف الصائتة والاتجاه
مصطلحات القسم (5)
Day & Bamford's principles
Ten principles for extensive reading programmes: easy, varied, self-selected, plentiful reading for pleasure.مبادئ القراءة الموسعة
Repeated reading
Reading the same short text several times to increase speed, accuracy and expression.القراءة المتكررة
Timed reading
Reading a text against the clock and recording reading rate to build fluency.القراءة المؤقتة
Synthetic phonics
Teaching individual letter–sound links first and then blending them into words.الصوتيات التركيبية
Analytic phonics
Teaching sound–spelling patterns by analysing whole words learners already know.الصوتيات التحليلية

Silent and oral readingالقراءة الصامتة والجهرية

  • Silent reading is faster and meaning-focused, so it builds reading fluency.الصامتة تبني الطلاقة
  • Oral reading mainly checks decoding and pronunciation; it is slower.الجهرية للدقة والنطق
مصطلحات القسم (2)
Silent reading
Reading without vocalising, focusing on meaning; it builds speed and comprehension.القراءة الصامتة
Oral reading
Reading aloud; used mainly to check decoding and pronunciation.القراءة الجهرية

أشهر الفخاخ في هذا الدرس

  • Looking up a word in a dictionary = scanning.القاموس = انتقائية
  • Choosing a title = skimming (reading for gist).العنوان = سريعة
  • Background knowledge + text clues = inferring; prior knowledge + title before reading = predicting.استنتاج / توقع
  • Intensive reading text = short and detailed, not long.المكثفة قصيرة
