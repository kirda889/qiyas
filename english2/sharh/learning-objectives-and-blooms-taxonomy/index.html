شرح الأهداف وتصنيف بلوم (Learning Objectives & Bloom's Taxonomy)

التصميم التعليميالمعيار 6.9.5التخصصي English 2 1448

Write measurable objectives, classify verbs on Bloom's levels and know the three domains.هدف قابل للقياس وتصنيف بلوم

Goals vs objectivesالغايات والأهداف

  • Goals are more general (long-term); objectives are specific, observable and measurable.الغاية أعمّ
  • 'Students will understand the plot' belongs under learning goals.تُكتب غاية
  • Key question: What should my students have learned by the end of this lesson?ماذا يتعلمون؟
  • General outcome: 'write academically'; specific outcome: 'use the present tense in reports'.عام / محدد
مصطلحات القسم (3)
Goal
A broad, long-term statement of intended learning.غاية
Objective
A specific, observable and measurable statement of what learners will do.هدف
Learning outcome
What learners are expected to be able to do after instruction.ناتج التعلم

Writing good objectivesكتابة الهدف الجيد

  • Objectives are SMART and begin with action verbs; conditions of good objectives — all EXCEPT general/broad.ذكي وبفعل إجرائي
  • Measurable: 'Students will pronounce /p/ and /b/' — NOT 'be familiar with sounds'.قابل للقياس
  • Good verbs: identify, list, distinguish, compare, write; avoid know, understand, appreciate.أفعال تصلح / لا تصلح
  • A good elementary objective: 'listen with the purpose of following directions'.اتباع التعليمات
  • Written by the teacher, shared before and during teaching; letting students personalize them raises motivation.يكتبها المعلم ويشرحها
مصطلحات القسم (4)
SMART
Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound.ذكي
ABCD model
Audience, Behavior, Condition, Degree.نموذج ABCD
Action verb
An observable verb such as identify, distinguish or write.فعل إجرائي
Behavioral objective
An objective stated in terms of observable learner behavior.هدف سلوكي

Bloom's taxonomyتصنيف بلوم

  • Bloom 1956: top level evaluation; revised 2001 (Anderson & Krathwohl): top level create.الأصلي: التقويم / المعدّل: الإبداع
  • Combining experience and new material into a new structure = synthesis; 'design an advertisement' = creating.تركيب / إبداع
  • Using procedures to solve tasks = application; categorizing with criteria = classifying; 'name three manners of articulation' = knowledge.تطبيق / تصنيف / معرفة
  • 'List' is lower-order; plan, assess and design are higher-order.يسرد أدنى
مصطلحات القسم (8)
Benjamin Bloom
Classified the cognitive domain of educational objectives.بلوم
Knowledge (remember)
Recalling facts: list, name, define.تذكّر
Comprehension (understand)
Explaining meaning: explain, summarize, paraphrase.فهم
Application
Using information in new situations: use, solve.تطبيق
Analysis
Breaking information into parts: compare, distinguish.تحليل
Synthesis
Combining elements into a new whole.تركيب
Evaluation
Judging value using criteria: judge, justify, critique.تقويم
Create
Producing original work: design, compose (highest in revised Bloom).إبداع

Three domains and other taxonomiesالمجالات الثلاثة

  • SOLO (Biggs & Collis): Structure of the Observed Learning Outcome — 5 levels.سولو 5 مستويات
مصطلحات القسم (5)
Cognitive domain
Objectives about knowledge and intellectual skills.معرفي
Affective domain
Objectives about attitudes, values and feelings (Krathwohl).وجداني
Psychomotor domain
Objectives about physical and motor skills.مهاري
Attitudinal objective
An objective targeting attitudes (e.g. developing a critical attitude).هدف اتجاهي
SOLO taxonomy
Prestructural, unistructural, multistructural, relational, extended abstract.تصنيف سولو

Unit-level objectives: terminal and enablingأهداف الوحدة: النهائية والتمكينية

  • Unit objectives = what learners achieve by the end of a unit; lesson objectives break them into smaller steps.هدف الوحدة يتفرع لأهداف دروس
  • Terminal objective = the final outcome (an 80-word invitation email); enabling objectives = the sub-skills leading to it.النهائي والتمكيني
  • Mager: performance (what) + condition (circumstances) + criterion (how well).أداء + شرط + معيار
  • Revised Bloom adds a knowledge dimension: factual, conceptual, procedural, metacognitive.بُعد المعرفة الأربعة
مصطلحات القسم (5)
Unit (module) objective
A learning objective for a whole unit, achieved through several lessons.هدف الوحدة
Terminal objective
The final performance expected at the end of a unit or course.هدف نهائي
Enabling objective
A sub-objective that leads to a terminal objective.هدف تمكيني
Mager's three components
Performance, condition and criterion of a behavioural objective.مكونات ماجر
Knowledge dimension
Revised Bloom's types of knowledge: factual, conceptual, procedural, metacognitive.بُعد المعرفة

Communicating objectives to learnersإيصال الأهداف للطلاب

  • Share objectives orally and in writing in student-friendly language.شفهياً وكتابياً وبلغة الطالب
  • Learning intentions = what students learn; success criteria = how they know they succeeded.نية التعلم ومعيار النجاح
  • Formats: WALT (We Are Learning To…), WILF (What I'm Looking For…), can-do statements.WALT وWILF
  • Learners self-check with exit tickets or traffic lights — linking objectives to self-assessment.بطاقة الخروج والتقييم الذاتي
  • CLIL or content lessons: a content objective plus a language objective (SIOP).هدف محتوى + هدف لغة
مصطلحات القسم (5)
Learning intention
A statement, shared with students, of what they are learning in a lesson.نية التعلم
Success criteria
Statements that tell students how they will recognise successful learning.معايير النجاح
WALT / WILF
'We Are Learning To' / 'What I'm Looking For': child-friendly frames for objectives and success criteria.صيغ الأهداف للطلاب
Exit ticket
A short task at the end of a lesson that shows whether the objective was achieved.بطاقة الخروج
Language objective
An objective stating the language learners will use to work with content (SIOP).هدف لغوي

Objectives in practiceالأهداف عملياً

  • Objectives link each lesson to the national standards; write them as learner outcomes, not teacher activities.الأهداف تربط الدرس بالمعايير
  • Explain → Understand; compare → Analyze; judge → Evaluate; design → Create.أفعال كل مستوى
مصطلحات القسم (1)
Time-bound objective
The T in SMART: the objective states when it will be achieved ('by the end of this 45-minute lesson').هدف محدد بزمن

أشهر الفخاخ في هذا الدرس

  • Highest level: evaluation (original) vs create (revised) — read which version the question means.انتبه للنسخة
  • Conditions of objectives: all except 'general/broad'.ما عدا عام
  • 'Explain a character's feelings' = comprehension (explain), not evaluation.يشرح = فهم
