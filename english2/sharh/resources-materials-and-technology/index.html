شرح مصادر التعلم والمواد والتقنية (Resources, Materials & Technology)

التصميم التعليميالمعيار 6.9.6التخصصي English 2 1448

Distinguish authentic from non-authentic materials, know adaptation, graphic organizers and learning technologies.المواد الأصيلة والتكييف والتقنية

Authentic materialsالمواد الأصيلة

  • Authentic materials serve real-world purposes (newspapers, web pages, menus).أصيلة
  • Customs of greeting, habitation and clothing are best presented through authentic materials.الثقافة بمواد أصيلة
  • Intermediate students preparing a presentation: the most appropriate sources are news websites.مواقع الأخبار
مصطلحات القسم (2)
Authentic materials
Materials created for native speakers for real communicative purposes.مواد أصيلة
Non-authentic (pedagogic) materials
Materials designed for teaching, e.g. textbooks and worksheets.غير أصيلة

Adapting and evaluating materialsتكييف المواد وتقييمها

  • No textbook is perfect: modify and supplement it for each lesson and class.عدّل وأضف
  • Trying a textbook in class to discover strengths and weaknesses = piloting.التجريب
  • Main selection criterion: the material fulfils learning goals.تحقيق الأهداف
مصطلحات القسم (2)
Materials adaptation
Adding, deleting, modifying, simplifying or reordering material.تكييف المواد
Piloting
Trying out a textbook in class to evaluate it.التجريب

Graphic organizers and visualsالمنظمات المرئية

  • Venn diagram → compare/contrast; flow chart → process steps; concept map → central idea; fishbone → cause and effect.أي منظم لأي غرض
  • A one-page visual of images, charts and minimal text = infographic.إنفوجرافيك
مصطلحات القسم (6)
Venn diagram
Overlapping circles for comparing and contrasting.مخطط فن
Flow chart
A diagram showing the steps of a process.مخطط انسيابي
Concept map
A diagram showing a central idea and related concepts.خريطة مفاهيم
Mind map
A visual web of ideas around a central topic.خريطة ذهنية
Infographic
A one-page visual combining images, charts and minimal text.إنفوجرافيك
KWL chart
Know / Want to know / Learned.جدول KWL

Learning technologiesالتقنية في التعليم

  • Blackboard is a virtual learning environment (VLE).بيئة تعلم افتراضية
  • Computers are best used as teacher aids; self-learning software helps independent learners.الحاسب مساعد
  • Synchronous = same time (Skype, live class); asynchronous = different times (email homework, blogs, wikis).متزامن / غير متزامن
  • Online + face-to-face = blended learning; a downside of web resources: technical problems.مدمج / المشاكل التقنية
  • Prezi = presentations; OCR = print to editable text (e.g. Braille); OneNote = real-time collaboration.أدوات
  • Evaluate CALL software by its use in the teaching and learning process; the first two SAMR levels enhance a task, the last two transform it.الاستخدام في التعليم، وSAMR: تحسين ثم تحويل
مصطلحات القسم (8)
CALL
Computer-Assisted Language Learning.التعلم بمساعدة الحاسب
Virtual learning environment (VLE / LMS)
An online platform for courses (Blackboard, Moodle).بيئة افتراضية
Synchronous learning
Learning that happens at the same time for all participants.متزامن
Asynchronous learning
Learning at different times and places.غير متزامن
Blended learning
Combining online and face-to-face instruction.تعلم مدمج
OCR
Optical Character Recognition: converts printed text to editable text.التعرف الضوئي على الحروف
SAMR
Substitution, Augmentation, Modification, Redefinition.نموذج سامر
TPACK
Technological, Pedagogical and Content Knowledge.إطار تيباك

Learner autonomyاستقلال المتعلم

  • Best way to encourage autonomy: train students to use reference resources.تدريبهم على المراجع
  • Tasks that help learners take control of their learning develop autonomy.تحكّم الطالب بتعلمه
مصطلحات القسم (1)
Learner autonomy
The learner's ability to take charge of their own learning.استقلال المتعلم

Techniques for adapting materialsأساليب تكييف المواد

  • Examples: more practice items = extending; a new pronunciation stage = expanding; vocabulary task before the reading = reordering.أمثلة تطبيقية
  • Coursebook evaluation: predictive (before use, e.g. Cunningsworth's checklist) or retrospective (after use).قبل الاستخدام أو بعده
مصطلحات القسم (7)
Adding (extending / expanding)
Supplementing material with more of the same, or with a new dimension.الإضافة
Deleting (subtracting / abridging)
Removing part of the material, either reducing it or omitting a whole section.الحذف
Modifying (rewriting / restructuring)
Changing the content or the classroom organisation of an activity.التعديل
Simplifying
Making a text or instructions easier (vocabulary, sentence length).التبسيط
Reordering
Changing the sequence of activities or units.إعادة الترتيب
Replacing
Substituting material with a more suitable alternative.الاستبدال
Predictive vs retrospective evaluation
Evaluating materials before use vs after using them.تقويم تنبؤي / استرجاعي

Resources that build independent learnersمصادر تبني المتعلم المستقل

  • Autonomy grows through learner training: dictionaries, vocabulary notebooks, goal-setting, self-evaluation.تدريب الطالب على التعلم
  • A self-access centre offers materials (graded readers, audio, answer keys) learners choose.مركز التعلم الذاتي
  • Learning contracts, learning logs and portfolios build responsibility; tasks with choice and answer keys allow self-correction.عقود وسجلات واختيار
مصطلحات القسم (4)
Learner training
Explicitly teaching learners strategies and skills for learning on their own.تدريب المتعلم
Self-access centre
A space or platform with materials learners use independently.مركز التعلم الذاتي
Learning contract
An agreement between learner and teacher on goals, tasks and evaluation.عقد التعلم
Learning log
A record in which learners note what they learned and how.سجل التعلم

Digital tools and platformsالأدوات والمنصات الرقمية

  • Madrasati (مدرستي): the Ministry of Education's national e-learning platform (2020) for classes, lessons, tests and parents.منصة وزارة التعليم الرسمية
  • Kahoot = game quizzes; Quizlet = flashcards; Padlet = digital wall; Edpuzzle = video with questions.أدوات صفية
  • MALL = learning with phones and tablets.التعلم بالجوال
  • Generative AI output must be checked by the teacher for accuracy, level and cultural fit.الذكاء الاصطناعي بإشراف المعلم
  • Digital citizenship: safety, privacy and copyright — prefer Creative Commons materials; judge any app by learning objectives first.المواطنة الرقمية والهدف أولاً
مصطلحات القسم (5)
Madrasati platform
The Saudi Ministry of Education's national learning management platform.منصة مدرستي
MALL
Mobile-Assisted Language Learning.التعلم بالأجهزة المحمولة
Interactive video
Video with embedded questions or notes that learners respond to (e.g. Edpuzzle).فيديو تفاعلي
Digital citizenship
Safe, responsible and ethical use of technology.المواطنة الرقمية
Creative Commons
Licences that let creators allow others to reuse their work under stated conditions.رخص المشاع الإبداعي

Digital writing toolsأدوات الكتابة الرقمية

  • Google Docs: several students write and edit one document at the same time.كتابة تعاونية
  • Grammarly checks grammar, spelling and style; Word's spelling checker, thesaurus and word count support writing.أدوات تدقيق الكتابة

Online platforms for skills practiceمنصات رقمية لممارسة المهارات

  • Text-based posts and blogs give practice in writing; video sites mainly support listening.المنشورات المكتوبة للكتابة
  • Online tutoring platforms connect learners with proficient speakers for live speaking practice.محادثة مباشرة
  • A vlog is a video blog; a vodcast is a video podcast — both recorded, one-way input.محتوى مسجل باتجاه واحد
مصطلحات القسم (3)
Vlog
A video blog: regular video posts by one creator.مدونة فيديو
Vodcast
A video podcast distributed as episodes.بودكاست مرئي
Online tutoring platform
A service that connects learners with tutors for live one-to-one lessons.منصة دروس مباشرة

أشهر الفخاخ في هذا الدرس

  • Web pages and newspapers are authentic; textbooks are not.الأصيل
  • Blackboard = virtual learning environment, not a social network.بيئة افتراضية
  • Email homework = asynchronous.غير متزامن
