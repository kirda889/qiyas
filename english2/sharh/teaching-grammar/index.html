Distinguish deductive from inductive teaching; know form–meaning–use and the main lesson frameworks.الاستنتاجي والاستقرائي وأطر الدروس

Deductive vs inductiveاستنتاجي واستقرائي

  • Deductive: the rule is presented first, then examples (rule → example).القاعدة ثم الأمثلة
  • Inductive: examples first; learners discover the rule (example → rule), e.g. guided discovery.الأمثلة ثم القاعدة
مصطلحات القسم (4)
Deductive approach
Presenting a rule first and then applying it in examples.استنتاجي
Inductive approach
Learners work out the rule from examples.استقرائي
Guided discovery
The teacher leads learners to discover rules through questions and examples.اكتشاف موجه
Explicit vs implicit teaching
Stating rules openly vs learning them without explanation.صريح / ضمني

Good grammar teachingالتدريس الجيد للقواعد

  • Larsen-Freeman: grammar = form, meaning and use.شكل ومعنى واستخدام
  • Grammar in context, practice to internalize, accepting developmental grammar; non-contextualized drills are NOT good practice.بلا سياق = ممارسة سيئة
  • Focus on form: brief attention inside a communicative task; focus on forms: discrete structures.على الشكل / على الأشكال
  • Consciousness-raising draws attention to language features; a gap-fill = controlled practice.رفع الوعي
مصطلحات القسم (5)
Form – meaning – use
Larsen-Freeman's three dimensions of grammar.الأبعاد الثلاثة
Focus on form (FonF)
Drawing attention to linguistic form during meaning-focused activity.التركيز على الشكل
Focus on forms
Teaching discrete grammatical structures one by one.التركيز على الأشكال
Consciousness-raising
Activities that make learners notice grammatical features.رفع الوعي
Structure-based instruction
Instruction focused on the language itself (vocabulary and grammar).تعليم قائم على البنية

Lesson frameworksأطر الدروس

  • PPP: Presentation → Practice → Production (the effective order).عرض ← ممارسة ← إنتاج
  • TTT: Test → Teach → Test; the first test finds out what students know.اختبار يكشف ما يعرفونه
  • ESA (Harmer): Engage → Study → Activate.شدّ، دراسة، تفعيل
  • CCQs check meaning; ICQs check instructions; timelines teach tenses.فحص المفهوم والتعليمات
مصطلحات القسم (5)
PPP
Presentation, Practice, Production.عرض، ممارسة، إنتاج
TTT
Test, Teach, Test.اختبار، تعليم، اختبار
ESA
Engage, Study, Activate (Harmer).إطار هارمر
CCQs
Concept-checking questions that verify understanding of meaning.أسئلة فحص المفهوم
ICQs
Instruction-checking questions that verify understanding of a task.أسئلة فحص التعليمات

Grammar points that appear in the examنقاط قواعد تتكرر

  • The present perfect is the hardest tense for beginners (no direct Arabic equivalent).المضارع التام الأصعب
  • 'I have broken it yesterday' ✗ — finished past time needs the past simple.وقت محدد = ماضٍ بسيط
  • Present perfect continuous: 'I have been working on this book for ten months.'مضارع تام مستمر
  • Comparing two pictures uses the present tense.مقارنة الصور = مضارع
  • 'The pupils you and me saw' → you and I (subject).you and I
مصطلحات القسم (2)
Present perfect
have/has + past participle; links past to present, no specific past time.مضارع تام
Present perfect continuous
have/has been + -ing; an action continuing up to now (for/since).مضارع تام مستمر

Teaching conditionalsتدريس الجمل الشرطية

  • Zero: If + present, present — general truths ('If you heat ice, it melts').حقائق عامة
  • First: If + present, will + base — real or likely future.مستقبل محتمل
  • Second: If + past, would + base — unreal present/future ('If I had a million riyals…').خيالي في الحاضر
  • Third: If + past perfect, would have + past participle — unreal past, regret.خيالي في الماضي
  • No will in the if-clause; CCQs: 'Did she study? (No) Did she pass? (No)'.لا will بعد if
مصطلحات القسم (5)
Zero conditional
If + present, present: general truths and facts.الشرط الصفري
First conditional
If + present, will + base form: real future possibility.الشرط الأول
Second conditional
If + past simple, would + base form: unreal present or future.الشرط الثاني
Third conditional
If + past perfect, would have + past participle: unreal past.الشرط الثالث
Mixed conditional
Combines times, e.g. past condition with present result ('If I had studied medicine, I would be a doctor now').شرط مختلط

Teaching reported speechتدريس الكلام المنقول

  • After a past reporting verb, tenses shift back (will → would; tomorrow → the next day).الرجوع خطوة في الزمن
  • Backshift is optional when still true: 'He said the sun rises in the east.'اختياري إذا بقي صحيحاً
  • tell needs a person object ('told me'); say does not.tell تحتاج مفعولاً
  • Reported questions: statement word order ('He asked where I lived'); yes/no → if/whether.ترتيب الخبر
مصطلحات القسم (3)
Backshift
Moving a verb one tense back in reported speech after a past reporting verb.الإرجاع الزمني
Reporting verb
A verb that introduces reported speech: say, tell, ask, explain, suggest.فعل النقل
Reported question
An indirect question with statement word order, introduced by a wh-word or if/whether.سؤال منقول

Teaching articles and countable/uncountable nounsتدريس أدوات التعريف والمعدود وغير المعدود

  • a/an by sound: an hour, a university; the = known, unique or specific.a/an حسب الصوت، وthe للمحدد
  • Zero article for general plural or uncountable nouns: 'Cats are clever.'بلا أداة للتعميم
  • 'The life is hard' = negative transfer from Arabic 'al-'.«ال» العامة من العربية
  • Uncountable: information, advice, news, homework — count with partitives (a piece of advice).لا جمع لها
  • many / a few + countable; much / a little + uncountable.many للمعدود
مصطلحات القسم (4)
Zero article
Using no article, e.g. with plural or uncountable nouns in general statements.غياب الأداة
Generic reference
Referring to a whole class of things rather than specific ones ('Dogs are loyal').إحالة عامة
Uncountable (mass) noun
A noun with no plural form that cannot take a/an (water, advice).اسم غير معدود
Partitive
An expression that counts an uncountable noun (a piece of, a cup of, a slice of).تعبير التجزئة

Teaching the passive and relative clausesتدريس المبني للمجهول وجمل الصلة

  • Passive = be + past participle, when the agent is unknown, unimportant or obvious.be + التصريف الثالث
  • Only transitive verbs have a passive: '*The accident was happened' ✗.لا مجهول للفعل اللازم
  • Teach the passive through process descriptions and news headlines.وصف العمليات والأخبار
  • Relative clauses: defining — no commas; non-defining — commas, no 'that'.المحددة بلا فواصل
  • Arab learners add a resumptive pronoun ('the book that I read it').الضمير العائد
مصطلحات القسم (4)
Agentless passive
A passive sentence without a by-phrase because the agent is unknown or unimportant.مجهول بلا فاعل
Defining relative clause
A relative clause that identifies which person or thing is meant; no commas.صلة محددة
Non-defining relative clause
A relative clause adding extra information, set off by commas; 'that' is not used.صلة غير محددة
Resumptive pronoun
An extra pronoun repeating the antecedent inside a relative clause ('*the man whom I met him').الضمير العائد

Current trends in grammar teachingاتجاهات حديثة في تدريس القواعد

  • Processing Instruction (VanPatten): structured input forces attention to form before production.مدخلات مُهيكلة قبل الإنتاج
  • Input enhancement (Sharwood Smith): bold, colour or underlining; input flood: many examples.إبراز الشكل والإغراق
  • Dictogloss: hear, note, reconstruct in groups — meaning plus form.إعادة بناء النص
  • All apply noticing (Schmidt).فرضية الملاحظة
مصطلحات القسم (4)
Processing Instruction
VanPatten's input-based approach that pushes learners to make correct form–meaning connections.تعليم المعالجة
Structured input
Input activities designed so that learners must process a target form to understand meaning.مدخلات مُهيكلة
Input enhancement
Making target forms more salient in input, e.g. by highlighting or bolding.تعزيز المدخلات
Input flood
Providing input containing many instances of a target form.إغراق المدخلات

Inside PPP and checking meaningداخل مراحل PPP والتحقق من المعنى

  • Presentation: new form in context — the stage with the most teacher control.العرض: أعلى تحكم
  • Practice: controlled activities (drills, gap-fills) for accuracy.الممارسة: دقة
  • Production: free communicative use for fluency; delayed feedback.الإنتاج: طلاقة
  • Grammar CCQs: 'You mustn't use your phone' → 'Is it allowed?'سؤال فحص للقواعد
مصطلحات القسم (2)
Controlled practice
Highly guided activities that focus on accurate use of the new form.تدريب موجه للدقة
Free practice (production)
Open activities in which learners use the new language freely for fluency.استخدام حر للطلاقة

أشهر الفخاخ في هذا الدرس

  • Rule first, then examples = deductive (even if options say 'declarative' or 'formal').استنتاجي
  • Non-contextualized drills are NOT good grammar practice.بلا سياق = سيئ
  • PPP order: presentation comes first, production last.العرض أولاً
