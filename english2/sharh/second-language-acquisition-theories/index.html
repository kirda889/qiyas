Krashen's Monitor Modelفرضيات كراشن الخمس

  • Krashen's Monitor Model: five linked hypotheses; fluency comes from subconscious acquisition, not learned rules.الطلاقة من الاكتساب لا من القواعد
  • Conscious error correction while speaking = the Monitor; over-users speak hesitantly.المراقب: محرر واعٍ
مصطلحات القسم (6)
Acquisition–Learning Hypothesis
Distinguishes subconscious acquisition from conscious learning.الاكتساب والتعلم
Monitor Hypothesis
Learned knowledge edits output when there is time, focus on form and knowledge of the rule.المراقب
Natural Order Hypothesis
Structures are acquired in a predictable sequence.الترتيب الطبيعي
Input Hypothesis (i+1)
Acquisition results from comprehensible input slightly above current level.المدخل المفهوم
Affective Filter Hypothesis
Emotional factors can block input from being acquired.المرشح الوجداني
Comprehensible input
Input learners can understand, with a little beyond their level.مدخل مفهوم

Later SLA theoriesنظريات بعد كراشن

  • Interaction Hypothesis (Long): negotiation of meaning makes input comprehensible.التفاعل: التفاوض على المعنى
  • Output Hypothesis (Swain): producing language reveals gaps — noticed most when learners talk to others.المخرج يكشف الفجوة
  • Noticing (Schmidt): input must be noticed to become intake.الملاحظة
  • Acculturation (Schumann): social and psychological distance from the target group.التثاقف والمسافة
  • Syntactic bootstrapping: inferring a word's meaning from its position and sentence structure.الإقلاع النحوي

Stages and L1 vs L2المراحل والفرق بين الاكتسابين

  • Early L2 learners show limited production (silent period).إنتاج محدود
  • L1 and L2 acquisition are alike: both occur through comprehensible input.المدخل المفهوم
  • L1 is instinctive, rapid and complete; adult L2 learners usually do not reach native-like grammatical competence.الكبير نادراً يصل للأصلي
  • Cummins: BICS (≈2 years) vs CALP (5–7 years).BICS / CALP
  • Knows the rule but says 'He run fast' → has competence but lacks performance; slips are performance errors.كفاية / أداء
مصطلحات القسم (5)
Silent period
An early stage with little or no L2 production.فترة الصمت
BICS
Basic Interpersonal Communicative Skills.مهارات التواصل الأساسية
CALP
Cognitive Academic Language Proficiency.الكفاءة الأكاديمية
Performance error
A slip in use despite knowing the rule.خطأ أداء
Metalinguistic awareness
Awareness of language as an object one can think about.الوعي بما وراء اللغة

Communicative competenceالكفاية التواصلية

  • Dell Hymes coined communicative competence: knowing what to say and how to say it appropriately.هايمز: المناسبة للموقف
  • Canale & Swain: grammatical, sociolinguistic, discourse and strategic competence (discourse added by Canale, 1983).المكونات الأربعة
  • Cohesive, coherent text (intersentential links) = discourse; 'an animal that lives in water' for 'fish' = strategic.خطابية / استراتيجية
  • Understanding the function of a spoken utterance = pragmatic competence.تداولية
مصطلحات القسم (7)
Communicative competence
Knowing how to use language appropriately in context (Hymes).الكفاية التواصلية
Grammatical competence
Knowledge of vocabulary, grammar and pronunciation rules.قواعدية
Sociolinguistic competence
Using language appropriately for the social context.اجتماعية لغوية
Discourse competence
Producing and understanding cohesive, coherent texts.خطابية
Strategic competence
Using strategies to compensate for gaps and repair breakdowns.استراتيجية
Pragmatic competence
Understanding the function and intention of utterances.تداولية
Circumlocution
Describing a word you don't know with other words.الشرح بكلمات أخرى

Stages of second language acquisitionمراحل اكتساب اللغة الثانية

  • Krashen & Terrell's Natural Approach: 3 early stages; teacher materials extend them to 5.ثلاث مراحل في الأصل وتُمدّ إلى خمس
  • Preproduction (silent period): understands, answers by pointing or nodding → TPR, pictures, yes/no questions.يفهم ولا يتكلم: TPR ونعم/لا
  • Early production: one- or two-word answers, chunks → either/or and who/what/where questions.كلمة أو كلمتان: أسئلة اختيار
  • Speech emergence: simple sentences, frequent errors → how/why questions, short stories, dialogues.جمل بسيطة: لماذا وكيف
  • Intermediate fluency: complex sentences, opinions → 'What would happen if…?'; advanced fluency ≈ Cummins' CALP.جمل مركبة وآراء ثم طلاقة أكاديمية
مصطلحات القسم (5)
Preproduction stage
The first SLA stage: comprehension with little or no speech.مرحلة ما قبل الإنتاج
Early production stage
Learners produce one- or two-word responses and memorised phrases.الإنتاج المبكر
Speech emergence stage
Learners produce simple sentences and short exchanges.ظهور الكلام
Intermediate fluency stage
Learners use more complex sentences and express opinions with fewer errors.الطلاقة المتوسطة
Advanced fluency stage
Learners approach native-like proficiency in social and academic language.الطلاقة المتقدمة

Learning strategies: Oxford (1990) and the good language learnerاستراتيجيات التعلم عند أكسفورد والمتعلم الجيد

  • Oxford's questionnaire is the SILL; O'Malley & Chamot use three groups: metacognitive, cognitive, social/affective.استبانة SILL
  • Good language learner (Rubin, 1975): guesses willingly, wants to communicate, is not inhibited by mistakes.صفات المتعلم الجيد
مصطلحات القسم (9)
Direct strategies
Oxford: memory, cognitive and compensation strategies that work directly with the language.استراتيجيات مباشرة
Indirect strategies
Oxford: metacognitive, affective and social strategies that support learning.استراتيجيات غير مباشرة
Memory strategies
Techniques for storing and retrieving new information (grouping, imagery, rhymes).استراتيجيات الذاكرة
Cognitive strategies
Mental operations on the language: practising, analysing, summarising.استراتيجيات معرفية
Compensation strategies
Strategies to overcome gaps in knowledge, such as guessing or using synonyms.استراتيجيات تعويضية
Metacognitive strategies
Planning, monitoring and evaluating one's own learning.استراتيجيات ما وراء المعرفة
Affective strategies
Managing emotions, anxiety and motivation.استراتيجيات وجدانية
Social strategies
Learning with and through others: asking questions, cooperating.استراتيجيات اجتماعية
SILL
Strategy Inventory for Language Learning: Oxford's strategy questionnaire.استبانة أكسفورد

More SLA perspectivesمنظورات أخرى في اكتساب اللغة الثانية

  • Skill Acquisition Theory (DeKeyser): declarative → procedural knowledge → automaticity through practice.من معرفة القاعدة إلى استعمالها تلقائياً
  • Investment (Norton): learners invest when a language promises symbolic and material resources; tied to identity.الاستثمار والهوية
  • Usage-based views: patterns are extracted gradually from frequent exposure to real use.التعلم من تكرار الاستعمال
مصطلحات القسم (4)
Skill Acquisition Theory
SLA as a skill moving from declarative to procedural knowledge and automaticity (DeKeyser).نظرية اكتساب المهارة
Declarative knowledge
Knowing about something: facts and rules that can be stated.معرفة تقريرية
Procedural knowledge
Knowing how to do something: the ability to use knowledge in performance.معرفة إجرائية
Investment (Norton)
A learner's commitment to learning a language, tied to identity and expected returns.الاستثمار

Self-repairتصحيح المتعلم لنفسه

  • Correcting your own slip while speaking ('I goed… went') shows metalinguistic awareness; the slip is a performance mistake.التصحيح الذاتي = وعي ما وراء لغوي
