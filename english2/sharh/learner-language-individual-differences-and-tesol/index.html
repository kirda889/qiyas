شرح لغة المتعلم والفروق وتعليم الإنجليزية (Learner Language, Individual Differences & TESOL)

Distinguish positive/negative transfer, interlingual/intralingual errors, interlanguage and fossilization; know TESOL terms.النقل والأخطاء واللغة البينية

Transfer and contrastive analysisالنقل والتحليل التقابلي

  • Contrastive Analysis (Lado, 1957) compares L1 with the target language to predict difficulties; linked to behaviorism.مقارنة اللغتين للتنبؤ بالصعوبة
  • Applying L1 knowledge to L2 = transfer; similar structures give correct output = positive transfer.نقل إيجابي
  • Negative transfer = the negative influence of L1 on L2: 'five years before', 'pen blue', 'the man whom I met him'.نقل سلبي
  • Teachers should analyze errors to find inappropriate L1 transfer, and expect learners to use their L1.حلّل الأخطاء
مصطلحات القسم (4)
Contrastive Analysis
Comparing L1 and L2 to predict learning difficulties (Lado).التحليل التقابلي
Transfer
Applying L1 knowledge to the L2.النقل
Positive transfer
L1–L2 similarity leads to correct L2 production.نقل إيجابي
Negative transfer (interference)
L1 patterns cause errors in L2.نقل سلبي

Error analysis and interlanguageتحليل الأخطاء واللغة البينية

  • Error Analysis (Corder): interlingual errors come from L1; intralingual errors come from the target language.من اللغة الأم / من اللغة الهدف
  • Errors such as 'rided' and 'goed' mean the student is learning (developmental pattern).أخطاء النمو علامة تعلم
  • Overgeneralization: 'writed', 'tooths', 'childs', 'swimmed'.تعميم زائد
  • The learner's developing system = interlanguage; incorrect forms that become permanent = fossilization.لغة بينية / تحجّر
  • Decrease in proficiency from disuse or L2 influence = attrition.تآكل
مصطلحات القسم (7)
Error analysis

Interlingual error

Loss of language proficiency over time.تآكل اللغة

Individual differencesالفروق الفردية

  • Age, personality, experience = individual factors; anxiety, self-restriction = affective factors.فردية / وجدانية
  • Technological factors are NOT a category of SLA factors.التقنية ليست منها
  • Fluency, accuracy and complexity = cognitive factors.معرفية
مصطلحات القسم (3)
Aptitude
A natural ability to learn languages (Carroll's MLAT).استعداد
Learning strategies (Oxford)
Memory, cognitive, compensation, metacognitive, affective, social.استراتيجيات التعلم
Affective factors
Emotional variables such as anxiety, self-esteem and inhibition.عوامل وجدانية

Bilingualism and TESOL termsالثنائية والمصطلحات

  • Fluent in two languages = bilingual; alternating between them = code-switching.ثنائي / تناوب
  • TESOL = Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages; TEFL = … as a Foreign Language; ESP = for Specific Purposes.الاختصارات
  • TESOL draws on theories of cognitive development to plan effective teaching.النمو المعرفي
  • Some see culture as the fifth skill; teach it starting from familiar local aspects.الثقافة المهارة الخامسة

Recent trends in TESOLالاتجاهات الحديثة في تعليم الإنجليزية

  • Shared direction: from native-speaker norms to intelligibility, intercultural skill and the learner's whole repertoire.من معيار الناطق الأصلي إلى الوضوح والتواصل
  • Native-speakerism (Holliday) = belief that native speakers teach better; Medgyes lists strengths of non-native teachers.قضية المعلم الناطق وغير الناطق
مصطلحات القسم (8)

English as a Lingua Franca (ELF)

Translanguaging
Using one's full linguistic repertoire, across languages, to make meaning and learn.التنقّل بين اللغات (توظيف المخزون اللغوي كاملاً)
Macrostrategies (Kumaravadivelu)
General post-method principles from which teachers derive classroom techniques.الاستراتيجيات الكبرى
Native-speakerism

Reflective teaching and action researchالتدريس التأملي والبحث الإجرائي

  • Reflective teaching: collect evidence about your own teaching (journals, recordings, peer observation) to improve it.التأمل في الممارسة لتحسينها
  • Schön: reflection-in-action = adjusting during the lesson; reflection-on-action = thinking back afterwards.التأمل أثناء الفعل وبعده
  • Action research: small-scale study of a problem in their own classroom; cycle plan → act → observe → reflect (Kemmis & McTaggart).دورة: خطط، نفّذ، لاحظ، تأمّل
مصطلحات القسم (4)
Reflective teaching
Systematically examining one's own teaching to improve it.التدريس التأملي
Reflection-in-action / on-action
Schön: thinking during an event vs thinking about it afterwards.التأمل أثناء / بعد
Action research
Cyclical, classroom-based research by teachers to solve a local problem.البحث الإجرائي
Peer observation
Teachers observing each other's lessons to give feedback and learn.ملاحظة الزملاء

TESOL and related disciplinesعلاقة تعليم الإنجليزية بالعلوم الأخرى

  • Psycholinguistics = language in the mind; sociolinguistics = language in society; neurolinguistics = language and the brain.علم اللغة النفسي والاجتماعي والعصبي
  • Educational psychology → motivation, assessment; anthropology → culture; corpus linguistics → word lists (corpora); computer science → CALL.علوم تغذي التدريس
  • CLIL teaches content through English: hard (strong) CLIL = a subject in English; soft (weak) CLIL = topic-based English course.التدريس عبر المحتوى
  • Cross-curricular links (science texts, maths word problems) give language a real purpose and build CALP.روابط بين المواد
مصطلحات القسم (5)
Psycholinguistics
The study of the mental processes involved in language use and acquisition.علم اللغة النفسي
Sociolinguistics
The study of language in relation to society and social factors.علم اللغة الاجتماعي
Corpus linguistics
The study of language through large computer-stored collections of real texts.لسانيات المدونات
Hard vs soft CLIL
Subject-led CLIL (content taught in English) vs language-led CLIL (language course using subject content).CLIL قوي / خفيف
Cross-curricular links

TESOL and cultureتعليم الإنجليزية والثقافة

  • Big C culture: literature, art, history, institutions; little c: customs, food, greetings, values.الثقافة الكبرى / اليومية
  • Cortazzi & Jin: materials present source culture (learners' own), target culture or international culture.ثقافة المصدر والهدف والدولية
  • Linguistic relativity (Sapir–Whorf): weak version (language influences thought) has support; strong version (determines) is rejected.النسبية اللغوية: الضعيفة مقبولة
  • Linguistic imperialism (Phillipson): the spread of English can reinforce inequality.الإمبريالية اللغوية
  • Good practice: compare cultures without judging, challenge stereotypes, start from local culture and Saudi values.المقارنة دون أحكام
مصطلحات القسم (6)
Big C culture
Formal culture: arts, literature, history and institutions.الثقافة الكبرى
Little c culture
Everyday culture: customs, routines, behaviour and values.الثقافة اليومية
Source / target / international culture
Cortazzi & Jin's three types of cultural content in materials.أنواع المحتوى الثقافي
Linguistic relativity
The Sapir–Whorf idea that language influences how speakers think and perceive.النسبية اللغوية
Linguistic imperialism
Phillipson's view that English dominance maintains structural inequalities.الإمبريالية اللغوية
Stereotype
An oversimplified, generalised belief about a group of people.صورة نمطية

From transfer to fossilizationمن النقل إلى التحجر

  • L1–L2 differences → negative transfer; repeated without noticing or feedback → fossilization.الفروق ← نقل سلبي ← تحجر

Reflection frameworks, avoidance and ESPأطر التأمل والتجنب والإنجليزية لأغراض خاصة

  • 5 R's of reflection: Reporting → Responding → Relating → Reasoning (theory, e.g. ZPD) → Reconstructing.وصف ← شعور ← ربط ← تفسير بالنظرية ← إعادة بناء
  • Avoidance: learners steer clear of difficult words or structures to avoid errors or embarrassment.تجنب التراكيب الصعبة
  • Workplace ESP courses are tailored to job-related needs (cabin crew, nurses, engineers).دورات مصممة لاحتياجات العمل
مصطلحات القسم (2)
5 R's of reflection
Reporting, Responding, Relating, Reasoning, Reconstructing: stages of structured reflection.مراحل التأمل الخمس
Avoidance
Not using difficult or unfamiliar language forms to escape errors.التجنب
