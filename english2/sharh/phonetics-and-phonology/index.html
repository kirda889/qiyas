Phonetics
The study of speech sounds and their production, transmission and perception.علم الأصوات
Phonology
The study of the sound system of a particular language.علم وظائف الأصوات
Articulatory phonetics
How speech sounds are produced by the speech organs.نطقي
Acoustic phonetics
The physical properties of speech sounds as sound waves.فيزيائي
Auditory phonetics
How speech sounds are heard and perceived.سمعي
Forensic phonetics
Identifying speakers and analysing recordings.جنائي
