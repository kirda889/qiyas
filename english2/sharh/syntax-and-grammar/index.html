Syntax and word classesالنحو وأقسام الكلام

  • Syntax studies the grammatical structure of sentences and the order of their words; the basic English word order is SVO (subject, verb, object), and every word belongs to a word class.النحو يدرس بنية الجملة وترتيب كلماتها، والترتيب الأساسي في الإنجليزية فاعل ثم فعل ثم مفعول.
  • English has two indefinite articles (a, an); this and that are demonstratives, and 'each other' is a reciprocal pronoun, not a reflexive one like myself.في الإنجليزية أداتا تنكير، والضمير each other تبادلي لا انعكاسي.
  • An adjective before a noun, as in 'an expensive watch', is attributive; after a linking verb, as in 'the watch is expensive', it is predicative.الصفة قبل الاسم صفة قبلية، وبعد فعل الربط صفة خبرية.
  • Adverbs modify verbs, adjectives and other adverbs but never nouns; very and too are intensifiers that strengthen an adjective or an adverb.الظرف يصف الفعل والصفة والظرف، ولا يصف الاسم أبداً.
  • 'Ouch!' is an interjection, a word that expresses emotion, while may, can and should are auxiliaries (modals) that add meanings such as possibility or obligation.أداة التعجب تعبّر عن الانفعال، أما (may, can, should) فأفعال مساعدة ناقصة.
